Greenfield Medicine Tampa is the clinic at 301 Harbour Place Drive, Tampa, FL 33602, in the Channel District. The phone is (845) 721-9458. The email on the site is doctor@greenfieldmedicine.com. The site describes help getting a Florida medical marijuana card: evaluations, certification, new patients, re-certifications, transfers, and card-status checks. It also offers a concierge visit at home.

The cost section prints a new-patient appointment fee of $210. It prints a renewal fee of $110. Read both numbers on greenfieldmedicine.com before you pay, because fees move.

## Two renewal sentences that do not match

One section says renewal occurs every 7 months. A FAQ on the same site says a medical marijuana card must be renewed annually. Both sentences are on the site. They cannot both be the rule you rely on. Call (845) 721-9458 or email doctor@greenfieldmedicine.com and ask which interval applies to a new certification versus a renewal. Do not pick one sentence and ignore the other.

The site says qualifying patients are enrolled with the Florida Office of Medical Marijuana Use Registry and that the card is mailed to the patient’s home address. It does not, in the text we saved, print how many days that mailing takes.

The FAQ gives examples of conditions the clinic says may qualify: chronic pain, cancer, epilepsy, glaucoma, PTSD, and multiple sclerosis. The same page tells readers to review the qualifying list and to contact the clinic if they believe a condition qualifies. Eligibility is a medical and state decision. This guide is not adding conditions the clinic did not name, and it is not telling you that you qualify.

## Questions to ask before a card appointment

This is general for any clinic that certifies a state card. It is not a description of an exam at Greenfield.

Ask whether the visit is in the office or the in-home concierge option, and which fee applies. The site lists both a clinic and in-home visits, and it lists $210 for a new patient. Ask whether the in-home visit is the same fee.

Ask what to bring: Florida residency papers, a photo ID, and records about the condition you want reviewed. The site does not print a document checklist in the section we saved. The clinic has to tell you.

Ask what happens if the doctor decides you do not qualify. The $210 line is a new-patient fee. The page does not say that fee is refunded.

Ask how a transfer from another physician works. Transfers are a named service. The steps are not spelled out in the homepage summary.

Ask the renewal question in the same call: every 7 months, or once a year, and is the renewal fee still $110.
