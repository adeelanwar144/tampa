## What to ask at the estimate

Ask which phone confirms the suite, how many sessions the estimate includes, and whether the $69 example applies to your size or only to the extra-small package. Ask whether a cosmetic brow session at $400 is a different appointment from a black-ink removal. Ask what the free estimate includes if you decide not to start.

A general planning note for laser removal, separate from this payment chart: cover the tattoo in a photo with a ruler before the consult so size talk is about your skin, not a guess. The page already sorts packages by square inches. Bring that photo. The page also says removal is uncomfortable. Plan the drive home after a first session rather than a same-day workout.

Read the [Westshore location page](https://removery.com/tattoo-removal/locations/fl/tampa/westshore/) again before you go. Package prices are marketing lines until the estimate has your name on it.
