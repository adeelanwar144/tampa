## What Copay It Forward means on this page

The page calls the program Copay It Forward and ties it to 340B pharmacy services. It says there has been $4M+ in free services for uninsured and underinsured patients since 2018, funded through that program and through insured patients. Listed benefits include free prescription delivery, same-day pickup, lower self-pay costs, and up to $100 per month in copay support for eligible patients. The page says those benefits are for patients receiving medical care from a METRO provider. If your prescriber is outside METRO, ask whether any of that list still applies.

If this pharmacy is the wrong fit, the page says there are 60+ partner pharmacies, including CVS, Walgreens, and Publix. Partner pickup is a different counter with different hours.

METRO describes HIPAA-compliant phone translation, including Spanish, French, and Creole, and ASL interpreting. The organization calls itself a 501(c)3, Metropolitan Charities, inc., doing business as Metro Healthy Communities, and it says it runs 100+ programs. Email on the page is info@metrotampabay.org. The pharmacy phone remains (813) 443-4854.

## What to ask with the prescription

Ask whether your prescriber is a METRO provider, whether delivery is actually free for your medication, and whether same-day pickup means the same afternoon or the next time the counter is open. Ask if the $100 copay figure applies to your drug. Ask for a translator when you book if you need Spanish, French, Creole, or ASL, because the page says those services exist and does not say they are standing at the counter with no notice.

A general planning note for any pharmacy handoff, separate from this program: bring the bottle, the insurance card, and a list of allergies. This page does not print a counseling script. The staff still need those facts.

Recheck the [locations page](https://metrotampabay.org/about/locations/metro-inclusive-pharmacy/) before a Saturday trip. Saturday is marked closed for this Tampa block.
