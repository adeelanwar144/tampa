Interbay Meat Market is the market at 6110 Interbay Blvd, Tampa, FL 33611, in Bayshore / Ballast Point. The phone is (813) 839-7542. The site calls the place a market, a deli, and “the Green Store,” the nickname it says locals use.

Store hours on the site are Monday through Friday 5:30am to 9pm, Saturday 6am to 6pm, and Sunday 8am to 4pm. Deli hours are shorter: Monday through Friday 5:30am to 8pm, Saturday 6am to 5pm, and Sunday closed. If you want a sandwich on Sunday, the deli line says closed even while the store lists 8am to 4pm. Call (813) 839-7542 if you need the counter rather than the grocery aisle.

## What the market says about itself

The page says the business has been family owned since 1985. It says the original building was 2,000 square feet and was built in 1942. It says Hani and Maria Shoubaki bought the market in 1985. It says the old store was demolished in 1998 and replaced by the current 10,000-square-foot building. It says Maria is originally from Havana, Cuba, and that the deli menu is Cuban and American.

The food it describes, without naming a sandwich list in the passage we used, is steaks, sausages made in-house, produce delivered fresh daily, and a grocery section for basic necessities. A later block on the same page drifts into unrelated casino copy. That block is not about the market and is ignored here. For the actual menu, use the site’s menu or the phone.

## Two clocks in one building

This is the practical part, and it comes from their own hours, not from a visit. The store and the deli do not share a closing time. A Friday night after 8pm can still be inside the store’s 9pm close and outside the deli. A Saturday after 5pm is the same split: store until 6pm, deli until 5pm. Sunday is the sharp one: deli closed, store listed until 4pm.

If you are ordering hot food, ask which clock you are on. If you are buying meat for a cookout, the page points you at steaks and in-house sausage and does not print a price per pound. Ask the counter.
