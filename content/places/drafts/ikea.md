A restaurant close at 7:00 PM is not a reason to assume the furniture floor has closed. A bistro that matches the store close is still a different counter.

## Click and collect, delivery, and the curb

Standard delivery is described as starting at $19 for IKEA Family members. Click and collect is free for IKEA Family members and $2 if you are not a member. The page says the store holds the order for 24 hours and then cancels it. It also says you need enough room in the vehicle for the order.

Curbside directions on the page: enter the property on Adamo Dr or N 26th St, follow the painted arrows to the left side of the building, park in a numbered spot, and have the last 4 digits ready for check-in. That is a pickup path, not a map of the customer parking for a walk-in visit. The page does not describe a garage. If you are walking the showroom, use 1103 N 22nd Street as the destination and ask where walk-in parking is when you arrive.

A general note about warehouse stores, not a measurement from this page: flat-pack furniture and a restaurant meal are different errands. Decide which one you are doing before you choose the 10:00 AM click-and-collect door or the 11:00 AM store door.
