## What to ask an audiology office

This is general, and it sits next to a real address conflict, so the first question is practical.

Ask which MacDill address to use: 310 or 10310. Repeat the suite. Ask whether Friday is still closed and whether the weekend is simply unlisted or actually shut.

Ask whether the visit is a hearing test, earwax removal, tinnitus care, or a hearing-aid fitting. The site lists all of them. A test and a fitting are different appointments.

Ask which manufacturer they are considering if you already know you want one of the named brands. A name on a website is not a promise that the model is in the office that day.

Ask what “financing” means before you agree to a device: which lender, what the payment is, and whether batteries and repairs are included. The site lists financing, batteries, and repair as separate lines.

If you are a musician asking about monitors or custom protection, say that when you book. Those are their own headings.

If you might be having a medical emergency, the site’s contact form says to call 911 rather than wait for business hours. That instruction is theirs.
