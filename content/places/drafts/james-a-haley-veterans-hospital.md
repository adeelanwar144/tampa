## A 24/7 campus, with clinics that do not all work the same way

James A. Haley Veterans' Hospital lists 13000 Bruce B. Downs Boulevard, Tampa, FL 33612-4745. That matches our listing at 13000 Bruce B Downs Blvd. The main phone on the VA page is 813-972-2000, the same number as our listing. Mental health is a different line, 813-631-7100. Use the main phone for the campus switchboard and the mental health phone when that is the clinic you need.

The facility hours are marked 24/7 every day.

The page also says hours may vary for different services and that you should select a service to check. Emergency room care is described as 24/7. A specialty clinic can still be appointment-only. "24/7" on the campus line is not a promise that audiology, prosthetics, or foot care will see a walk-in at midnight.

## What the main campus says it provides

The page says the campus provides 24/7 emergency room care, primary care, and specialty care, including cardiology, foot care, mental health care, treatment for spinal cord injuries, and prosthetics. Some services say you may visit with or without an appointment. Others say by appointment only, or that a referral is required. Read the service block you need. A primary-care rule is not a cardiology rule.

Wheelchairs are available when you arrive if you need one to get into the building. DAV vans are offered through Disabled American Veterans and county Veterans Affairs directors, and the page says individual van schedules vary. A weekday shuttle is mentioned. Ask the transportation desk which of those you qualify for. Beneficiary travel is described as its own benefit with its own rules.

## How to plan the trip

Call 813-972-2000 and name the service. Ask whether that clinic is walk-in, appointment-only, or referral-only. Ask about wheelchair availability if you need one at the door. Ask which van or shuttle runs on your day, because the page says those schedules vary. For mental health, use 813-631-7100 rather than assuming the main desk is the same queue.

A general planning note for a large VA hospital, separate from this campus map: bring your Veterans Health Identification Card and the name of the clinic, and allow time for parking even when the emergency entrance is obvious. The page points to a parking map and a campus map. It does not assign you a space in this article. If the need is an emergency, the page's own 24/7 emergency room line is the relevant door. If the need is a routine specialty, check that service's hours before you go.

The source to reread is the [VA location page](https://www.va.gov/tampa-health-care/locations/james-a-haley-veterans-hospital/). Service hours can differ from the campus 24/7 mark.

## Main line, mental health line, and the maps

13000 Bruce B. Downs Boulevard, Tampa, FL 33612-4745 is the campus address. 813-972-2000 is the main phone and matches our listing. 813-631-7100 is the mental health phone. Start with the clinic name, then pick the line. Emergency room care is the service the page marks as 24/7 along with the campus itself. Cardiology, foot care, spinal cord injury treatment, prosthetics, and primary care are named on the same overview, and each can still say appointment-only or referral-required once you open that service.

Wheelchairs are available at arrival for patients who need them to enter. DAV vans depend on Disabled American Veterans and county directors, and the page says each van schedule varies. A weekday shuttle is a separate mention. Beneficiary travel, mileage, and special mode transport are described as benefits you have to qualify for, not as a standing ride. Ask the transportation office which one applies to you.

Cano Health is 0.38 miles away and Aldi is 0.4 miles away, both on University Plaza. They are outside the campus. Do not look for a VA clinic inside either one. Use the parking map the page links, and allow time even for the emergency entrance. Bring the identification the VA expects for care. If you are in crisis, the page also points to the Veterans Crisis Line, which is a different conversation from a routine clinic booking.

The useful question is specific: "Is cardiology walk-in, or does it need a referral?" Swap in foot care, prosthetics, or mental health as needed. A campus marked 24/7 can still send you home from a specialty desk that is appointment-only. Read the service block the same day you travel.
