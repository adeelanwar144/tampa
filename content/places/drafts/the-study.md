## What to settle before a color, cut, or extension visit

This section is general advice for booking a salon that offers custom color, extensions, or a precision cut. It is not a description of how The Study Salon ran a particular appointment.

Ask who the time is with, and whether that person does the service you want. A cut, a color, and an extension appointment are different kinds of work at most salons, and they rarely take the same length of time. Ask how long the reservation blocks, and what they want you to do if you are running late.

Ask what “custom color” means for your booking. An all-over color, a root retouch, highlights, and a color correction are different jobs. If you have a photo, send it before the day so the stylist can say whether the reserved time matches the photo. If your hair was colored recently, say so when you book. Many salons need that history before they can say whether the work fits in one visit.

Ask which products will be used. This salon says it uses Maria Nila. If you have had a reaction to a product, say that when you text, not after you are already in the chair.

The site says the salon does extensions. It does not name a method or a brand of extension on the pages we read. Ask which method they are proposing, what the upkeep is, and whether a consultation is separate from the install. Those answers change the appointment, and they have to come from the salon.

Ask how they want a cancellation. The site does not print a cancellation window.
