## Before you book a lift

Tell them the truck year and whether you want a leveling kit, a lift, tires, or an alignment by itself. Ask how long the truck stays, whether the $500 or $886 wheel is still the one in the photo, and whether Sunday by appointment includes the install or only a conversation. Ask where to park a lifted truck if the bay is full.

A general planning note for a lift install, separate from this page: a wheel price is not the out-the-door total once sensors, alignment, and labor are added. This site mentions alignment and in-house install. It does not print a labor menu. Get that number on the estimate.
