Frank's Clothier is the formal-wear shop at 4343 West Kennedy Blvd, Tampa, FL 33609, in Beach Park / MacDill. The phone is (813) 286-7670. The email on the site is Valentin@franksclothier.com. The site asks you to book a one-on-one appointment rather than treat the door as a walk-in counter.

The page describes custom-made suits cut to your measurements, off-the-rack suits and tuxedos, alterations, refined casual wear, and rentals. It does not print a price for any of those. A quoted number has to come from the appointment.

## The clock they publish

Frank's Clothier prints a fitting clock, not a restaurant week. Weekday appointments can be booked from 10:00 AM until 6:00 PM. The Kennedy Boulevard shop stops Saturday appointments at 3:00 PM. Sunday has no fitting at all. A tuxedo rental for a Sunday wedding still has to be measured on one of those open days, and Valentin@franksclothier.com is the mailbox the site gives you for that booking.

## What to settle before a fitting

This is general advice for a shop that sells custom suits, rentals, and alterations. It is not a report of a fitting at Kennedy Boulevard.

Ask whether the visit is a measurement, a rental pickup, or an alteration drop-off. Those are different lengths of time. A custom suit and a same-week rental are not the same job. The site lists both. Ask which one the appointment is for, and what you should bring: shoes you will wear, a date, and any suit you already own if the visit is an alteration.

Ask how far ahead a rental tuxedo needs to be reserved, and when it has to come back. The site does not print a rental window. Get that from Valentin or whoever books the chair.

Ask what “custom-made to your measurements” includes on the day you book: one jacket, trousers, a shirt, or a full set. The page lists the category and does not itemize a package.

If you are shopping off the rack, ask whether the Saturday 3:00 PM close still leaves time for a tailor mark. Alterations are named as their own service. They may be a second visit.
