## What to have ready for a branch visit

This is general advice for walking into a bank branch. It is not a list of Hancock Whitney’s requirements, because the location page does not print one.

Ask which of the named services can be opened or changed at this lobby, and which ones are handled at the wealth office or online. The location paragraph names checking, savings, cards, loans, mortgages, and wealth and private banking. It does not say every one of those can be finished at the teller line on a walk-in visit.

Bring whatever identification you would bring to open or change an account, and ask before you go if a business visit needs different paperwork than a personal one. The page says the center serves personal, small business, and commercial customers. It does not say those are the same appointment.

If you need a loan or a mortgage, ask whether this lobby takes the application or only starts it. The page says loans and mortgages are among the services. It also says accounts and loans are subject to credit approval. Approval, a rate, and a timeline are questions for the banker, not figures this page can supply.

If you need the ATM rather than a person, the page says it is open 24 hours. It does not say which networks it charges, or whether the lobby door is the ATM door. Look at the Suite 150 note if you are there after 4:00pm, when the lobby hours say the lobby is closed, and call (800) 448-8812 if the walk-up is the deposit you actually need.
