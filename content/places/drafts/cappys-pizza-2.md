## What to decide before you order

This part is general advice for ordering from a shop that offers more than one crust and also offers delivery. It is not a report of a meal.

Pick the crust before you pick the toppings. A Chicago deep dish and a New York thin crust bake differently, and they do not always share a ready time. If one person wants deep dish and someone else wants thin, ask whether those can leave the kitchen together. Ask the restaurant, not a guess on this page.

Ask what “vegan” covers on the night you order. At many pizza shops the crust, the cheese, and the sides are separate questions. A vegan cheese does not automatically make a side vegan. Cappy’s says vegan options exist. The details are on the menu we did not copy, or with the person who answers (813) 238-1516.

If you are using delivery, ask what the delivery area is before you build the order. The homepage announces delivery. It does not print a map, a fee, or a minimum on the page we read. Those belong to the order screen or to a phone call.

If you are doing takeout, ask how they will tell you the pizza is ready, and what name to give. The site has a takeout label. It does not print a pickup window or a parking spot for people who are waiting.

Ask about classic sides the same way. The homepage names them and does not list them. Salads are a labeled menu group, not a described dish.
