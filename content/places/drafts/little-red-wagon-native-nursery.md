## Shop hours and garden hours

Shop doors: 11:00am to 5:00pm most days, and 9:00am to 5:00pm on Saturday.

Garden and exhibits open at 11:30am, or at 9:30am on Saturday, and last admission is 4:00pm.

A 5:00pm shop close is not permission to enter the garden at the last minute. The page is explicit about last admission.

No weekly hours were stored in our database. The website block is the schedule to use, and you should still reread it, because the page itself labels the block as new hours.

## What you can buy, and what you cannot have shipped

The site says it does not offer shipping or delivery. Online orders are for a pickup date you choose. Staff set the items aside and notify you before that pickup. If a plant is out of stock, the plant shop menu has a custom order form. Single-day tickets are purchased at the registers, not described as an online ticket in the hours note.

Departments named on the site include plants and collections, a bug zoo and garden, a gift shop, books and stationery, a children's corner, garden and outdoor goods, home goods, and personal care and apparel. Example prices visible on the shop include plants from $7.00 and from $18.00. Those are examples, not a full price list. Ask before you assume a vine is in stock.

The mission line is "nurture native landscapes." The purpose line is to promote beneficial native plants through education, outreach, and help for landowners. The site says proceeds benefit the Tampa Bay Butterfly Foundation for conservation, education, and research. That is the nursery's statement about where the money goes.
