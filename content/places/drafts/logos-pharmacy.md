## What the pharmacy says it is

The homepage calls Logos RX a multi-state licensed 503A compounding pharmacy with sterile and non-sterile compounding labs. It says the pharmacy serves Tampa Bay and ships to patients across multiple U.S. states. Cities named as service areas include Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Lutz, Palm Harbor, and Largo. A provider page says clinic accounts are reviewed before someone can see the catalog and pricing. There is a link to report an adverse reaction. This guide does not repeat individual formulas or doses from the catalog.

A general note about compounding pharmacies, not a clinical instruction: a retail question and a provider order are different conversations. If you are a patient, ask whether your prescription has to come through a clinic account. If you are a prescriber, the page says the account has to be verified first.

## Two clocks, plus chat

Retail is listed 9:00am to 5:00pm on weekdays.

Online ordering is listed 8:00am to 7:00pm EST, and that line includes Saturday. Chat is marked 24/7.

The retail sentence never says the Waters Avenue counter is closed on the weekend, and it never says it is open. Ask.

Our stored hours only name Wednesday. The website retail line is the Monday-through-Friday span. Do not treat a single stored day as proof that Tuesday is different.
