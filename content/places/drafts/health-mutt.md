## What the site lets you do there

Three services are labeled: pet grooming, a self-service dog wash, and walk-in nail trims. The nail-trim label says they are daily. Departments named are dog food and supplies, and cat food and supplies. The dog-wash line on the site is “You make the mess, we clean it up.”

A weekly clock was not in the homepage text we read. “Daily” on a nail-trim label is not a substitute for open and close times. Call (813) 231-3137 or reopen tampahealthmutt.com before you drive to Florida Avenue with a wet dog and a deadline.

## Questions for a pet wash or a grooming slot

This is general for a shop that offers both a self-service wash and a grooming appointment. It is not a visit to Health Mutt.

Ask which one you are booking. A self-service wash means you do the bathing. A grooming slot means the shop does. The site lists both. They will not take the same amount of time, and a walk-in nail trim may be a third, shorter stop. The site says nail trims are walk-in and daily. It does not say a full groom is walk-in. Ask.

Ask what you should bring: your own towel, a lead, or nothing. The wash slogan says the shop cleans the mess. It does not list a tool kit.

Ask which cat foods and dog foods are actually on the shelf for the age and size you need. Health Mutt says the team will help you choose and that the price should fit your needs. The homepage never printed that price. Get it before anyone opens a large bag.

If your pet is nervous around other dogs, say so. A self-service wash in a shared shop is a different setting from a private groom. The site does not describe the room. The staff can.
