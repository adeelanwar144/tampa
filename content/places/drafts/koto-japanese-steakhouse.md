The site describes Japanese and Asian dishes, hibachi cooked at the table, and sushi. It says the restaurant has been established for over 10 years. ## Lunch, then a gap, then dinner

Weekday lunch is Monday through Friday 11:30am to 3:00pm. Dinner is Monday through Thursday 5:00pm to 10:00pm, Friday 5:00pm to 11:30pm, Saturday 3:00pm to 11:30pm, and Sunday 3:00pm to 10:30pm. On Monday through Friday the site does not list the stretch between 3:00pm and 5:00pm as open. Do not arrive in that weekday gap and assume the hibachi is running. Saturday and Sunday start at 3:00pm and do not show a separate lunch line.

## Delivery, birthdays, and combinations

Delivery is listed with a $30 minimum and a limit of 4 miles. Promotions are dine-in only and cannot be combined with another special. The birthday line is a free birthday meal up to a $15 discount, with ID shown to the server. If you want that discount, ask before you order, and do not stack it on another deal. The site says the combination is not allowed.

The page does not print a hibachi price or a sushi list in the passage we used. Open Koto’s menu for those names and prices.

## Sharing a table at a hibachi

This part is general for a steakhouse that cooks at the table. It is not a review of a meal on Howard.

Ask whether you want the hibachi or the sushi bar, because the site offers both and they are different seats. Ask how many people the birthday discount covers. The line says a meal up to $15. It does not say the whole table eats free.

Ask whether delivery can include the birthday offer. The site says promotions are dine-in only, so a delivered birthday meal is a question, not an assumption.

If someone in the party cannot sit at a shared grill, say so when you book. The site describes a flaming tableside presentation. It does not describe a private room.
