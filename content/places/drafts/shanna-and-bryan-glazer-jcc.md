## Howard Avenue, and no weekly chart on the homepage

The Shanna and Bryan Glazer JCC lists 522 N. Howard Ave, Tampa, FL 33606. That matches our listing. The phones on the page are 813.575.5900 and (813) 575-5900, the same number written two ways. Email is info@ShannaandBryanGlazerJCC.com. Our neighborhood label is [West Tampa](/neighborhoods/west-tampa). The homepage does not print a weekly open-and-close chart. Call a membership specialist or the main number and ask for the building hours before you drive over for a class.

The page says the JCC is more than a gym. Members are told they can use fitness and aquatics facilities, a gymnasium, and an indoor track, plus fitness, mind/body, educational, recreational, and cultural programs. It says all faiths and backgrounds are welcome. That welcome line is on the homepage. It does not spell out a guest fee.

## What to ask a membership specialist

Ask the building hours for the day you want, whether fitness and the pool share one membership, and whether a class such as Parkinson's fitness or swim lessons needs a separate signup. Ask about preschool and camp dates if that is the visit. Ask about wedding and bar or bat mitzvah rental if you need the event venue. Ask whether a non-member can buy a day visit. The homepage does not print that price.

A general planning note for a community-center membership, separate from this menu: decide which room you will actually use before you compare a price you have not been quoted.
