## Questions worth asking any shop before you approve a repair

This section is general. It is not a story about a car left at Robertson Street.

Ask for the diagnosis in plain language, and ask which of the published groups it falls under: maintenance, brakes, engine and drivetrain, transmission, air conditioning, body, or a fleet account. A named group on a website is not the same thing as a written estimate for your vehicle.

Ask what “scheduled service” means for your mileage. Shops use that phrase for very different lists. Oil and fluids are named here. Filters, tires, and inspections are not named as their own lines, so ask if you think you need them.

Ask whether a transmission job is a fluid service, a rebuild, or a replacement. The site lists all three. They are not the same bill.

Ask whether body work is a paint touch-up, a dent, or collision restoration. The site lists all three. Insurance handling is not described on the pages we read. If a claim is involved, ask the shop how they want that handled before the car is dropped off.

For a fleet, ask what the “program” includes. The site says commercial fleets can be maintained. It does not print a contract, a pickup schedule, or a vehicle count.
