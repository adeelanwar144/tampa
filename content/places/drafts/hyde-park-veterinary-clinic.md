## Regular hours, plus three holiday dates

Main hours are Monday through Friday 7:30am to 6:00pm, Saturday 8:30am to 12:00pm, and Sunday closed. An early drop-off has to fit that 7:30am open. Saturday ends at noon.

The same page lists holiday closures: 11/26/26 closed, 12/25/26 closed, and 01/01/27 closed. Those dates are what the page showed. Holiday schedules change. Read hydeparkvet.com again if your visit is near one of them.

If a pet needs care outside those hours, the site says to contact Blue Pearl at the nearest location. It does not give a street for that hospital. This page will not invent one. In an emergency, use the clinic’s instruction or emergency services, and do not wait on a closed Sunday door at Swann Avenue.

## What to ask before you book

This is general advice for a veterinary appointment. It is not a chart from this clinic.

Ask whether the slot is an exam, a food or medicine refill, or something that needs a longer visit. The site offers booking and an order path for food and medicine. Those can be different trips.

Ask which records to send ahead, and whether the PetOne app is required or optional. The site names the app. It does not say every client must use it.

Ask what to do if your pet is anxious in a clinic. The homepage has a link about stress and comfort. The advice itself is on that linked page, which is the clinic’s, not a summary invented here. Open it if that is the issue you are walking in with.

Ask about the Saturday noon close if you work weekdays. 8:30am to 12:00pm is a short window.
