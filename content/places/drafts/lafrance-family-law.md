## How to prepare for a family-law consult

This is general. It is not a file at 2503 W Swann Ave.

Ask which of the named paths fits: mediation, collaborative divorce, an uncontested case, or a contested one. The site lists all four. They are not the same process and they do not take the same amount of time.

Ask whether custody, support, or alimony is part of the consult, or whether the meeting is only about divorce paperwork. Bring dates, the other parent’s or spouse’s name, and any existing order you already have. The site does not print a document list.

Ask what the child support calculator is using, and whether the lawyer wants you to run it before the meeting or leave it alone. A public calculator and a lawyer’s advice are different things.

If the matter involves the military, parental rights, guardianship, domestic violence, or an appeal, say that when you call. Each of those is a separate heading on the site. The person who answers can tell you whether the consult should be scheduled differently.

Ask which of the two phone numbers is the right one for a new client. Having both printed is a reason to confirm, not a reason to pick at random.
