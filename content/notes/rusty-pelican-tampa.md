# Rusty Pelican Tampa: My Honest Rocky Point Guide

Rusty Pelican Tampa is the waterfront seafood restaurant on Rocky Point—fresh Gulf seafood, surf and turf, tropical ambiance, and bay views. People go for brunch by the water, a romantic dinner, a late-night meal after landing near [Tampa International Airport](/getting-around/tampa-international-airport), or a private event with a Tampa Bay backdrop. Visit Tampa Bay files the cuisine as Seafood and the style as Upscale Casual, and highlights plates like Key West Black Grouper and Crispy Whole Local Snapper plus Midwestern beef cuts with Caribbean flair on local seafood.

Address: **2425 N Rocky Point Drive, Tampa, FL 33607**. Phone **(813) 281-1943**. Book through the restaurant’s [reservations page](https://www.therustypelicantampa.com/reservations). **Last updated:** November 2, 2026.

## First Things First

- **Bay views and seafood first**—then confirm you booked Tampa, not Miami.
- **Brunch Sat–Sun; happy hour in the bar/lounge on weekdays** (see hours below).
- **Self-park and valet** are both listed; put the full street number in the rideshare.
- **Reservations accepted**—use the restaurant’s own hours when directories disagree.

## Location Mix-Up (Tampa vs Miami, TPA vs Terminal)

The same brand name points at a different restaurant in Miami / Key Biscayne: 3201 Rickenbacker Causeway, Key Biscayne, FL 33149, phone (305) 361-3818, different hours. Booking the Key Biscayne sunset and then driving to TPA because the name matched ruins the night.

Visit Tampa Bay places the Tampa room minutes from Tampa International, minutes from downtown, and between Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Pasco. That is **airport-adjacent**, not inside a terminal. You still need a car, rideshare, or hotel shuttle. Rocky Point is west of downtown—not Ybor, not Water Street.

Bahama Breeze at 3045 N Rocky Point Drive East is a different driveway. Put **2425** in the reservation note.

For Westshore shopping before dinner, [International Plaza](/shopping/international-plaza-tampa) is the nearby mall people mean.

## View and Atmosphere

Visit Tampa Bay describes breathtaking views of Tampa Bay and a waterfront banquet, dining, and event facility. The restaurant pitches tropical ambiance for romantic dinners, brunch by the water, and late-night meals. Style: **Upscale Casual**. Wheelchair accessible per Visit Tampa Bay. Event spaces include outdoor patios and a ceremony gazebo; the Bay Room is described with fireplace, stained-glass, bay views, private entry, and up to **50** guests.

## Where People Struggle

First, the Miami restaurant. Second, treating “minutes from TPA” like a terminal walk. Third, hours wars between directories and the restaurant. Fourth, picking the wrong Rocky Point driveway. Fifth, planning a seated dinner off contradictory capacity fields instead of calling events.

It is not Bern’s on South Howard, and it is not a hotel restaurant inside TPA. If what you really wanted was “nice restaurants near my airport hotel,” say the Rocky Point address out loud before you confirm. For a churrascaria night instead of Rocky Point seafood, see [Terra Gaucha](/food-and-drink/terra-gaucha-tampa) or [Texas de Brazil](/food-and-drink/texas-de-brazil-tampa). Downtown Water Street after a Channelside day is a different errand—[Yard House](/food-and-drink/yard-house-tampa) sits in that cluster.

## FAQ

### Is Rusty Pelican Tampa the same as the Miami Rusty Pelican?

No. Tampa is at 2425 N Rocky Point Drive, phone (813) 281-1943. Miami/Key Biscayne is a separate restaurant on Rickenbacker Causeway.

### Do they have brunch and happy hour?

Brunch Saturday–Sunday 10:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. on the reservations hours. Happy hour is weekday bar/lounge service—see the happy-hour menu page for Mon–Fri 4:00–6:30 p.m. Bar and Lounge only.

### How much does it cost?

Visit Tampa Bay’s average entree figure is $28. Confirm live menu prices at the restaurant.

### Is there parking?

Yes—self-park and valet per Visit Tampa Bay. Confirm valet pricing on site.

### What should I wear?

Upscale casual (Visit Tampa Bay style listing).
