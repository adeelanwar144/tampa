Notes on portillo's tampa fl, and which door that actually is

People say portillo's tampa fl, portillo's tampa florida, portillo's tampa, tampa portillo's, portillo's in tampa florida, portillo tampa, and portillos in tampa. Portillo's itself does not collapse those into one building. The one with a Tampa address is Portillo's Tampa at 2102 E. Fowler Avenue, Tampa, FL 33612. Phone (813) 540-9001. That is the store I mean when someone says Tampa and not a suburb. There are more.

The Fowler store

Portillo's lists drive thru, delivery, kiosk ordering, and an Americana theme for this store. They call it a 1930s Prohibition themed restaurant. Chicago street food. They name char-broiled burgers, Italian beef sandwiches, Chicago-style hot dogs, a fresh chopped salad, and the chocolate cake shake. They say open late, family dining, delivery and catering.

Restaurant hours and drive thru hours are the same. Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 a.m. Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Holiday hours may vary. I would not assume Christmas matches a Friday. I also would not assume the drive thru stays open after the dining room. As they give them, the two clocks match.

They give distances from that Fowler address: 1.0 mile from the University of South Florida campus, 2.7 miles from Adventure Island, 3.2 miles from Busch Gardens, 4.8 miles from ZooTampa at Lowry Park, 9.1 miles from Amalie Arena. Those are their figures, not a drive I timed. The useful part for me is which trip the store belongs to. Fowler is the north Tampa store, the one you use if the day is USF or the parks on that side of town. It is not a Hyde Park lunch and it is not a downtown walk from the river.

Portillo's says this Tampa restaurant debuted September 26, 2016, their second Florida location and their 45th location overall, 1930s Prohibition theme, Chicago-style hot dogs, Italian beef, burgers, salads, and chocolate cake, at 2102 E. Fowler Avenue. I am using that date as the one they announced, not as something I watched.

What else is in the Tampa area

I checked four Portillo's locations. More than one of them will come up if you say portillo's tampa fl and let the map decide.

Portillo's Brandon, 1748 W. Brandon Boulevard, Brandon, FL 33511. Phone (813) 210-8190. Same feature list: drive thru, delivery, kiosk, Americana. They also call it 1930s Prohibition. Same hours I found at Fowler, dining room and drive thru, holiday hours may vary. They give 1.6 miles from Brandon Exchange, 3.1 miles from Topgolf Tampa, 6.5 miles from the Florida State Fairgrounds, 8.4 miles from Seminole Hard Rock. Brandon is not the City of Tampa. It takes an 813 number and it is the store people mean when they are already on the Brandon side. An older Portillo's announcement called Brandon the first Florida location, on the northwest corner of SR 60 and Lakewood Drive, said they expected to be serving in March of 2016, and mentioned a double drive-thru. What Portillo's says about Brandon now is a drive thru, without the word double, so I am not promising two lanes. Portillo's calls Fowler the second Florida store, which fits Brandon coming first. I still would not tell someone the Brandon first day was a specific March date. They said they expected March.

Portillo's (Lutz) Wesley Chapel, 26000 State Road 56 W, Lutz, FL 33559. Portillo's gives that address and a short line about Chicago-style hot dogs, Italian beef, burgers, salads, and chocolate cake. They did not give a phone number and they did not give hours. I am not filling those in from the Fowler clock. If the trip is Wesley Chapel or Lutz, I would check again the day I go, because what I got was thin. The store's name is both Lutz and Wesley Chapel, and the street address is Lutz. That is enough to send someone to the wrong exit if they only remember "the new one by the highway."

Portillo's St. Petersburg, 2302 Tyrone Boulevard North, St. Petersburg, FL 33710. Phone (727) 390-6625. This one is not a Tampa address and the phone is 727, not 813. Features: drive thru, delivery, kiosk, and a diner theme. They describe it as a 1950s-60s diner, not the 1930s Prohibition look at Fowler and Brandon. Same hours pattern as the other two full stores: 10:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, 10:00 a.m. to midnight Friday and Saturday, drive thru matching, holiday hours may vary. They give distances to Walter Fuller Park, Tropicana Field, John's Pass, and Pasadena Golf and Yacht Club. If someone says portillo's tampa fl and the pin that comes back is Tyrone Boulevard, that is the wrong side of the bay. I would not treat St. Pete as a Tampa lunch unless I meant to drive it.

I did not look into a Riverview restaurant, or a Clearwater one, or a Sarasota one. I am not saying those can never exist. I am saying the stores I actually checked were Fowler, Brandon, Lutz/Wesley Chapel, and St. Petersburg. A business registered with Portillo in its name in Riverview is not this restaurant, and I am not using it.

The menu, without prices I did not see

The company menu is grouped as Hot Dogs, Beef and Sausage Sandwiches, Chicken, Char-Broiled Burgers, Sandwiches and Ribs, Salads, Barnelli's Pasta, and Sides and Soup. I could not get item names or prices under those headings. So I am not inventing a hot dog topping list or a shake price. What I will stand on is how Portillo's describes each store, which repeats the same foods: char-broiled burgers, Italian beef sandwiches, Chicago-style hot dogs, chopped salad, chocolate cake shake. Fowler, Brandon, and the short Lutz description all say chocolate cake. Portillo's announcement of the Tampa debut says chocolate cake too. That is the dessert I would expect to see on the board. I would read the board for the number.

How people get the wrong store

The name is the problem. Portillo's tampa fl sounds like one address. The map will offer Brandon because Brandon is close and the name Portillo's is the same. It will offer Lutz if you are north. It will offer St. Petersburg if you are sloppy about the bay. Fowler is the only one of those four whose address says Tampa, FL.

The second problem is the kind of meal. This is not a reservation steakhouse. Nothing I came across asked for a credit card to hold a table. The features are drive thru, kiosk ordering, delivery, catering. You can sit down. Portillo's sells family dining and a theme, Prohibition at Fowler, diner at St. Pete. You can also never leave the car. If I send a group and tell them to meet inside at 8 on a Saturday, I would still tell them the drive thru is open until midnight that night and that a kiosk is how the inside order works. I did not find a host stand policy, a waitlist, or a dress code. I would not invent one. Ball caps are not a Bern's problem here.

The third problem is the holiday caveat. Every store with full hours says holiday hours may vary, on both the dining room and the drive thru. A park day that lands on a holiday needs a check that morning. I am not going to guess they close early.

The fourth problem is which theme you wanted for a photo. Fowler and Brandon are the 1930s rooms by Portillo's own description. St. Pete is the 1950s-60s diner. If someone is trying to match a picture, the wrong city is also the wrong decade.

How I would use it

For portillo's tampa fl I would go to 2102 E. Fowler Avenue and call (813) 540-9001 if I needed the holiday clock. I would expect Italian beef, a Chicago dog, a char-broiled burger, a chopped salad, and a chocolate cake shake, ordered at a kiosk or a drive thru that, on a normal week, matches the dining room hours. I would use Brandon, 1748 W. Brandon Boulevard, (813) 210-8190, only if I was already on that side. I would not send a Wesley Chapel plan off what little Portillo's gives for Lutz until they give hours. I would not cross to Tyrone Boulevard in St. Petersburg and call it the Tampa store. One chain, more than one Tampa-area door, and only Fowler is the Tampa address.
