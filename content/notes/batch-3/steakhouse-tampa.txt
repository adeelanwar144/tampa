Notes on steakhouse tampa

People ask for steakhouse tampa, best steakhouse in tampa, tampa steakhouse, best steakhouse tampa, steakhouse in tampa, steakhouse in tampa fl, and steakhouse in tampa florida. Best does not sort anything. Asking that way gets you a Howard Avenue institution, a Hyde Park Village room, a Westshore wood-fire room that is about to move, two different addresses facing International Plaza, a Brazilian churrasco on Boy Scout, and an American grill on Dale Mabry that is not only a steakhouse. I looked into each of those on this pass. I am not ranking them. I am trying to keep the doors from collapsing into one dinner.

Bern's, and the clock that argues with itself

Bern's is the room people want when they mean the old one. 1208 S Howard Avenue, Tampa, FL 33606. Phone (813) 251-2421. Contact email bshcontact@bernssteakhouse.com. Bern's dates its steaks, wine, and dessert room to 1956. Steaks cut to order, dry-aged in house five to eight weeks. Oysters, escargot, twenty-two caviars. Wine over half a million bottles and 6,500 labels, a 50-degree cellar plus their warehouse, a 2016 James Beard award for the wine program, more than 150 by the glass.

The hours are where I would not trust any single statement from them. Their standard posted hours, repeated in several places, are Sunday 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm, Tuesday through Thursday 5:00 to 10:00, Friday and Saturday 5:00 to 11:00, Monday closed. Their contact information says Sunday to Thursday 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm and Friday and Saturday 5:00 to 11:00, which reads as if Monday is open, and it still carries a line about temporary hours for the rest of March, 4:00 pm to 9:00 pm daily. I am writing this in October. A March notice still attached to their contact details is not a plan I would drive on. Their reservation policy says, in its own line, that Bern's is closed on Mondays. If I had to bet, I would bet on the standard hours and that reservation line, and I would still call, because the contact details have not been cleaned up.

The reservation policy settles the rest. Rolling sixty days, reservations open at 8:00 am, a map app only shows thirty days, a card holds the reservation, and the contact email does not book anything. Parties of nine and up go through group dining, ninety days out. No callback list. The special-request note is not read until the night of. They only seat complete parties. Dress is business casual to semi-formal, and relaxed clothes may be sent to the lounge. No self-parking. Valet is available. Dinner already includes a seat in the Harry Waugh Dessert Room if you tell the server. Dessert-only, by phone, only from 6:00 to 6:45 pm. I never saw a menu with steak prices, so I am not writing one.

Howard Avenue at dinner is its own errand. It is not the mall, and it is not Boy Scout Boulevard.

The International Plaza pile

Fleming's Tampa: 4342 W Boy Scout Blvd, Tampa, FL 33607, 813-874-9463. Seven minutes from the airport, across from International Plaza, in their words. A wine vault of 2,500 bottles. Main dining room Monday through Saturday 11:00 am to 10:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am to 9:00 pm. They post holiday hours, including Christmas Day closed. Social hour 3:00 to 6:30 pm daily. Live music every night, they say. Chef partner Brandon Grady, operating partner Jorge Diaz. This one does lunch. Bern's, on the hours I trust more, does not.

Ocean Prime Tampa is the other International Plaza door, and it is not the same corner even though both answers sound like "by the mall." 2205 N. Westshore Boulevard, Tampa, FL 33607, phone (813) 490-5288, at the entrance to the shopping center on the corner of West Shore and Boy Scout. Their hours: lunch Monday through Friday 12:00 to 4:00 pm, dinner Monday through Friday 4:00 to 10:00 pm, Saturday 3:00 to 10:00 pm, Sunday 5:00 to 9:00 pm. Lounge happy hour Monday through Friday 4:00 to 6:00 pm. They pitch surf and turf, seafood and prime steak, and they mention a private-label oyster. A person who wants a steakhouse and needs a fish-and-steak room with a weekday lunch is closer to this one, or to Fleming's, than to Howard Avenue. A person who has a 1:00 pm lunch at Fleming's and a 1:00 pm lunch at Ocean Prime is still looking at two buildings on two sides of the same mall.

Charley's Tampa says it has been at 4444 West Cypress Street, Tampa, FL 33607 for nearly thirty years, phone (813) 353-9706. USDA Prime and Choice, hand-cut, on a 1,200-degree pit fired with Florida citrus and oak. Sunday through Thursday 5:00 pm to 9:30 pm, Friday and Saturday 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm. Private rooms up to fifty. Valet. Charley's also says that in 2027 they move less than a mile, to West Spruce and North Westshore, across from International Plaza. Until then, Cypress. Charley's also has Celebration and Orlando locations, so a convention-center pin can be the wrong city. The Tampa restaurant says a nearly 500-selection wine list. The brand as a whole says over 800. I am not merging those numbers.

Texas de Brazil is the same Boy Scout corridor and a different format. 4112 West Boy Scout Boulevard, Tampa, FL 33607, 813-981-7157. They post set prices: dinner $65.99, salad-area only $33.99, Monday through Thursday 5:00 to 9:30 pm, Friday 5:00 to 10:00, Saturday 4:00 to 10:00, Sunday 4:00 to 9:00. Weekend lunch uses the dinner price, Saturday noon to 4:00 and Sunday 11:00 to 4:00. Weekday lunch is $39.99, salad area $23.99, Monday through Friday 11:00 to 2:00. Those prices leave out alcohol, featured items, desserts, tax, and gratuity. Self-parking. Parties over fifteen should call. A single ribeye off an a-la-carte menu is the wrong expectation. It sits near Fleming's and Ocean Prime and is not those rooms.

Hyde Park, which is not Howard Avenue either

Meat Market Tampa: 1606 W Snow Avenue, Tampa, FL 33606, 813-280-1113. Hyde Park Village, not Bern's on South Howard, even though both come up as South Tampa steak. Lunch Monday through Friday 11:30 am to 3:00 pm. Brunch Saturday and Sunday 11:00 am to 3:30 pm. Happy hour daily 4:00 to 7:00 pm. Dinner Sunday through Wednesday 5:00 to 11:00 pm, Thursday through Saturday 5:00 pm to midnight. Valet Wednesday through Friday from 11:30 am, and Saturday and Sunday from 11:00 am. A lounge Friday and Saturday from 5:00 pm. They call it 6,000 square feet and have a separate dress code. I never got the details of that dress code, so I am not inventing the fabric rules. Beach clothes are a gamble.

The room that says steak and is not only a steakhouse

J. Alexander's Tampa comes up whenever people ask about steakhouses, and the company itself does not pretend to be Bern's. 913 N Dale Mabry Hwy, Tampa, FL 33609, (813) 354-9006, Monday through Sunday 11:00 am to 10:00 pm. Just off Dale Mabry near International Plaza. Wood-fired steaks, seafood, cocktails, desserts. Their dining guidelines say most seating is for fours and cannot be combined. Their dress code is business or smart casual, and they name tank tops, open-toed shoes, loungewear, and athletic clothes as out. A lot next to the building. This is a seven-day lunch-through-dinner American room on Dale Mabry. It is closer, as a landmark, to the International Plaza cluster than to Howard or Snow, and it is still a third building, not Fleming's and not Ocean Prime.

What I thought people were struggling with

They add best and expect one address. These restaurants sort into at least three geographies: Howard Avenue for Bern's, Snow Avenue for Meat Market, and the Westshore and Boy Scout and Westshore Boulevard knot for Fleming's, Ocean Prime, Charley's current Cypress Street room, Charley's future Westshore door, Texas de Brazil, and, a few blocks off the mall on Dale Mabry, J. Alexander's. They assume every steakhouse is dinner only. Fleming's, Ocean Prime, Meat Market, Texas de Brazil, and J. Alexander's all have a midday service. Bern's and Charley's, on the hours I noted, do not. They assume Monday is open because Bern's contact details say Sunday to Thursday. Bern's standard hours and reservation policy say Monday is closed, and a stale March notice is still hanging around. They assume International Plaza means one steakhouse. I counted more than one door that uses that mall as the landmark, plus a mall gravity that pulls Charley's toward it in 2027 while the steaks are still on Cypress. They assume a steakhouse check is one piece of meat at a market price. Texas de Brazil posts a set price and a cheaper salad-area price and tells parties over fifteen to call. They assume valet is universal. Bern's says there is no self-parking. Texas de Brazil says self-parking is available. Meat Market posts the days valet starts.

I am not picking a best steakhouse in Tampa. Asking for "best" cannot, either. It can only be kept straight by the street.
