Notes on nueva cantina - downtown tampa

People write it with the hyphen and without it. I wanted to know if "downtown Tampa" was a real door or a tag on the whole brand. It is a real door. Downtown is one of four locations, not a nickname for all of them.

The downtown door

Nueva Cantina is consistent on this. Downtown Tampa is 903 N Franklin St, Tampa, FL 33602. Phone 813-392-1007. Hours: Sunday through Thursday 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to midnight. That is the only address they label downtown. I am using those clocks because the restaurant gives them the same way every time.

The other three are how people end up at the wrong one. St. Petersburg is 1625 4th St S, St. Petersburg, FL 33701, phone 727-201-9183, Sunday through Thursday 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Brandon is 10033 E Adamo Dr, Tampa, FL 33619, phone 813-284-6884, Sunday through Thursday 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Palm Harbor is 35903 US Hwy 19 N, Palm Harbor, FL 34684, phone 727-216-6172, same hours as Brandon. Brandon has a Tampa ZIP and the word Tampa, and it is not Franklin Street. Palm Harbor is the palms problem. If someone says palms, that is a different Nueva, on US 19, with a 727 phone. South, as in 4th St S, is St. Pete, not a south room of the downtown restaurant.

I did not find Westshore, Ybor, or Channelside as a Nueva address. I did not find this place inside a Hilton, a Marriott, an inn, a suite hotel, or an apartment. They present it as its own restaurant at 903 N Franklin Street. Fort Brooke Garage, at 107 N Franklin Street, also comes up when people talk about downtown parking. Same street name, different number. I would not follow a Franklin pin until it says 903.

What they are selling

They lead with tacos and margaritas, and they say a limited Halloween menu runs until November 1st. I was looking in early October, so the end date was still ahead of me. I would not expect those Halloween names in December. The standing sections are tacos, margaritas, starters, bowlritos, pitchers, shareables, and kids.

Happy hour is Monday through Friday, 2 p.m. to 6 p.m., at all locations. Two tacos for 7 dollars, birria or steak an extra dollar. Nueva margaritas 7. Mocktails 5. Drafts, wells, and wines 4. Downtown opens at 11, so the afternoon window sits inside the day. It does not cover a Friday that runs to midnight. At 8 p.m. I would not expect the 7 dollar taco.

The food menu is one brand menu. I did not find a downtown-only menu. Prices here are what their menu gave. If a dish had no number, I am not inventing one.

Street tacos with prices: grilled chicken 4.50, pork carnitas 4.50, al pastor 4.75, birria 5.75, costra grilled cheese 6.75. Al pastor, grilled chicken, pork carnitas, and beef barbacoa are marked gluten free among the tacos. The menu still says flour tortillas contain wheat, the kitchen handles gluten, cross-contact may occur, and they cannot guarantee any item is gluten-free. I would tell them, and I would not treat the mark as a promise.

A street taco meal is 10.00 for two tacos plus rice and beans, beef or seafood 2 dollars more each, birria or costra cheese 3 dollars more each. A birria taco meal is 17.00 for two tacos with consomé, rice, and beans. Adding consomé to one birria taco is plus 3 dollars, and consomé as a side is 2.00. I would ask which cup they mean before I assumed those were the same thing.

Burrito and bowlrito are both 11.00, beef or seafood plus 4. The bowlrito is the burrito in a bowl. That word confuses people, and it is a menu item, not a place. Elote 6.50, esquites 5.50. Nueva nachos 15.00. Nueva salad 9.75. Carne asada plate 26.00. Taco tour 50.00 for 12 tacos. Nueva pizza and the Mexican pizza 20.00. Baja tostada 8.50. Quesadilla 9.50. Kids, ages 12 and under: nuggets 8.00, kidsadilla 6.00, churros 8.00, tres leches 8.00. A Halloween churro on the limited menu is 9.99, which is not the regular churro price.

Drinks, only where a price sat next to the name. Passionfruit, strawberry, and mango margaritas 12.00, pitcher 47. The spicy sports-ball margarita 13.00. Mojito 13.00. Michelada 13.00. Espresso ducktini 14.00. Several Halloween margaritas 14.00, and the soft-serve margarita 13.00. Sangria 11.00, pitcher 40. Classic pitcher 56.00. The Nueva margarita says make it a pitcher 39, and the skinny one says make it a pitcher 52. I am not filling in the single-drink price when the menu left it off. Michelob Ultra draft 5.00. Modelo Especial bottle 5.00. Beer bucket 19.00 for five. Horchata, strawberry horchata, and pineapple agua fresca 4.25. Mexican Coke 4.50. The fountain sodas I saw are 3.25. Jarritos 3.95. The mocktails that had a price are 7.00. Parties of 6 or more get 20 percent gratuity. That is at the bottom of the menu, so a downtown six-top is not a surprise I can shrug off.

Groups and catering

For reservations they offer seating for large groups from 10 to 30 or more, with the Franklin address and the 813 number. The first large-party request form says minimum 10. Brandon and Palm Harbor say minimum 6. I will not promise downtown is 10, and I will not promise it is 6. For this door I would call 813-392-1007. St. Pete's 727 number is easy to grab by mistake. Calling the first phone number you see is how I would book the wrong city.

For private events they say any location, up to 50 guests, with a Downtown Tampa request form. Fifty in a private area is not the same sentence as groups of 10 to 30-plus. I would ask which room they mean.

Catering has its own downtown request form. Rice and beans are not included. Taco bar: 12 dollars for grilled chicken, pork carnitas, or veggies; 13 for asada or al pastor; 14 for barbacoa, shrimp, or fish. Fajita bar: 13 and 14 on the same split. Starters each serve 8 to 10, including chips 10, guacamole 18, queso 16, salsa 10, plantains, esquites, and elote 35, nachos 30. Sides in that range include beans at 30 and rice at 25. House and skinny margaritas are 10 dollars and say ID required. Disposable plates and napkins are 1 dollar a person. A chafing kit is 15 dollars, and they say the lighter is not included. Their catering intro names St. Petersburg, Tampa, or Brandon, while Palm Harbor also has a form. "Tampa" in that sentence is not automatically 903 N Franklin. I would pick the Downtown Tampa form on purpose.

What I would not assume

I would not use St. Pete hours downtown. Downtown is the late one, midnight on Friday and Saturday, against St. Pete at 10 p.m., Brandon and Palm Harbor at 11, and St. Pete at 9 p.m. Sunday through Thursday. I would not drive to Palm Harbor because someone said palms, or to Brandon because the ZIP says Tampa. I would not expect happy hour after 6. I would not expect the Halloween names after November 1. I would not invent a price for the beef barbacoa taco, the asada taco, or the shrimp and fish tacos, because those had no number even though the meals and catering price the proteins as add-ons.

Flour tortillas have wheat. A gluten-free mark is not a guarantee. Six people means the 20 percent is already in the check. The door I meant is 903 N Franklin Street, 33602, 813-392-1007, and I would say downtown Tampa out loud so the form does not slide me to 4th Street South.
