Notes on mid florida credit union amphitheater tampa

The name people use is close, and the spelling on the venue is tighter. The venue calls itself the MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre, one word, credit union in all caps the way the sponsor writes it. People say amphitheater and write mid florida as two words. Same place. It sits on the Florida State Fairgrounds. The address the venue gives is 4802 US Highway 301 North, Tampa, FL 33610. The fairgrounds use 4800 US Highway 301 North, Tampa, FL 33610 for the property, and when the fairgrounds talk about the amphitheatre they repeat 4802. I am treating 4802 as the venue door and 4800 as the fairgrounds office address, not as two different sheds.

It is not inside the City of Tampa, even though every official address says Tampa. The building is in East Lake-Orient Park, on the fairgrounds, next to Interstate 4. That community is an unincorporated census-designated place in Hillsborough County, home of the fairgrounds, bordered by the city of Tampa to the south and west. Its ZIP is 33610, which matches the venue. Interstate 4 runs west from there about 8 miles to downtown Tampa. The mailing address says Tampa. The ground is outside the city limits. I would not call it a downtown venue.

Who runs it

Live Nation manages the building. The credit union has held the naming rights since 2013, and it calls the venue Tampa's open-air Live Nation amphitheatre and says brokering tickets is not part of the sponsorship. They do not sell the seats. They point you to Live Nation. The phone that keeps coming up is 813-740-2446. The venue email is the Live Nation address for this amphitheatre, and the box office email is the Tampa Live Nation box office address. The fairgrounds main line, 813-621-7821, weekdays 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., is for the grounds, not for a concert ticket. I mixed those up once in my notes and separated them.

Because the name has changed so many times, I tracked the former names: Tampa Bay Amphitheatre while it was planned, Ford Amphitheatre from 2004 to 2010, 1-800-ASK-GARY from 2010 to 2012, Live Nation Amphitheatre early in 2013, then MidFlorida. Owner is the Florida State Fair Authority. Operator is Live Nation. It debuted in 2004, outdoor, capacity about 20,000, roughly 9,900 reserved and 10,000 on the lawn. I did not measure that. There is a decibel limit and a noise wall because people live close. Unincorporated does not mean empty.

The roof, which is the current annoyance

The venue says the roof reconstruction is taking longer than they thought, and that they are offering complimentary sunscreen and free-water hydration stations. The credit union says supply shortages and construction delays slowed a roof redesign, and that a full 2026 concert season is still on. Nobody gave me a finish date. I am not inventing one. The comfort plan they are advertising is sunscreen and water, not a completed cover.

Parking, which is not included

Parking is sold separately from the concert ticket. The venue says general parking is $20 per vehicle plus tax if you buy it in advance, and $25 on the event day. Lots open two hours before the scheduled gate time. Premier parking and VIP parking are the easier in-and-out options. General parking enters from US-301 or MLK Boulevard. Premier is described as the front of the lot until showtime, so arriving late does not shove you to the back if you bought that pass. I did not get a dollar figure for premier or VIP. I am not guessing it.

Accessible parking uses the Orient Road entrance. You need a visible state-issued plate or placard, the spot they describe is 9 by 18 feet, and oversized vehicles do not get that spot. No placard means MLK or 301 and general parking. First come.

Drop-off is at the Martin Luther King Boulevard entrance in the general lot. Tell parking staff you are dropping off. For pickup, the venue says arrive 45 minutes before the end of the show or you will not be allowed in. That is a harsh rule and I am glad they spelled it out, because I would have shown up at the encore.

Box office hours do not match

The venue says the box office opens on show days at 3 p.m. Its early October 2026 shows also open the box office and general parking at 3:00 p.m. The venue's FAQ answer says the same, day-of-show only, 3:00 p.m.

The credit union says the box office opens at noon on day of show only. I am not averaging that to 1:30. The venue says 3 p.m. The sponsor says noon. If I needed a will-call ticket I would believe the venue and still get there with slack, because the two official-ish clocks disagree.

Gates are not the box office. The venue says you can usually arrive 60 to 90 minutes before showtime, then tells you to check the specific event. Event-specific times come out closer to the date. I am not freezing one October bill into a house rule.

What they will not let in

The not-allowed list is long, and they say it can change by show: weapons, alcohol, drugs including marijuana, outside lawn chairs, umbrellas, glass, cans, aluminum bottles, CamelBaks, coolers, laser pointers, selfie sticks, animals except service animals, fireworks, outside food and drinks beyond the water rule, strollers, drones, aerosols, professional cameras, glow sticks.

What is allowed, in the general rule: a clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, or a small clutch that does not have to be clear, up to 6 by 9. Bags get checked. One factory-sealed bottle of water up to one gallon. Refillable plastic bottles are permitted. Camelbacks, tumblers, and aluminum bottles are not. YETI stations refill plastic bottles and cups for free. Water fountains sit in the east and west plazas outside the main restrooms.

I noticed the lawn-chair rules seem to fight each other until you hear both. The venue reminds you not to forget to reserve a lawn chair. It also says no outside lawn chair comes into the building, rentals are available for most shows, you turn them in at the end, and the rental chair is 27.6 inches wide, 27.6 deep, and 30.5 tall. So the chair you reserve is theirs, not the one in your trunk.

A show can tighten the water rule. For a bill on October 3, 2026, the venue said refillable bottles, metal or plastic, were not permitted for that show, sealed water no larger than 16 ounces, and it flagged an elevated heat event. The general gallon rule was not the rule for that night. I would check the event's own rules the day of, not the general FAQ from last month.

Other day-of facts

The venue is cash-free. An information booth will turn cash into a card, and they say that exchange is free. Tickets are mobile, downloaded in the Live Nation app before you leave. The show goes on rain or shine unless it is severe. Umbrellas are banned because they block sightlines. Guest services, in the eastern plaza, is lost and found during the show, and a designated driver can get a free fountain soda there. Merch is in the north and south plazas, with extras some nights.

Stage Left Club is a private bar, restrooms, and its own entrance, and it opens when the gates do. The Backyard is a VIP open-air lounge with cookout-style barbecue, lawn games, shaded seating, and local DJ or artist sets. I did not get a price for either. I am not writing one.

Members do not get a ticket discount. They may get presales and giveaways. Presales, when they run, are generally 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. the day before the public on-sale. I am not copying codes. They expire.

What I thought people were struggling with

People say tampa, and the venue puts a Tampa ZIP on an unincorporated fairgrounds. The credit union name is on the building, and Live Nation has the tickets, the parking passes, and the bag rules. Calling the credit union to buy a seat is the wrong desk. The credit union says so itself. Lawn chairs are a rental. Water might be a gallon or 16 ounces. The roof is not done. Parking is separate, $20 plus tax ahead or $25 the day of. The box office clock is 3 p.m. according to the venue and noon according to the sponsor. I am trusting the venue and writing down the disagreement.

What I am willing to say

MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre at the Florida State Fairgrounds, 4802 US Highway 301 North, Tampa, FL 33610, is the venue. It is outside Tampa city limits, in unincorporated East Lake-Orient Park, which is why the Tampa address keeps fooling people. Live Nation runs the shows. The 2026 season was still on, with a roof project unfinished, when I looked into it. I am not inventing a ticket price or a gate time for a show I did not see scheduled.
