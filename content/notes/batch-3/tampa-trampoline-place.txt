What tampa trampoline place actually is

I expected tampa trampoline place to be either a brand by that name or one building. There is no park called Trampoline Place. The phrase is generic, and it splits into more than one business. I am not inventing a second Tampa Altitude. I am writing down the addresses the businesses actually give.

Altitude, the Hillsborough one

Altitude Tampa, which the company tags as Drew Park, is the park people already mean when they say the Hillsborough Avenue trampoline place. Drew Park is the neighborhood, not a second building. Altitude gives the address as 4340 West Hillsborough Ave, Tampa, Florida 33614, and elsewhere as 4340 West Hillsborough Ave, Suite 350, Tampa, Florida 33614. The street and ZIP match. The suite does not appear in the shorter version. I would put Suite 350 in the map pin and still look for the door.

They give two phones, (813) 399-1529 and (813) 503-4428, and an email, chesley@altitudetampa.com. Nobody told me which number is the front desk. Their posted hours, which I would not trust on a holiday: Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Friday 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Saturday 9:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

What Altitude names as actually in this building: Basketball Trampoline Dunk, Battle Beam, Extreme Dodgeball, Kid's Court, Main Court, Performance Trampoline, Trapeze, Tumble Track, and ZipLine. Programs they name for it: Birthday Party On, Glo in the Park, Jump Happy Hour, and Junior Jumpers Jam. Most of what the company says publicly is generic copy about dodgeball, battle beam, air bags, and parties, plus a warning that programs differ by park. I am not adding a foam pit or a ninja wall just because other Altitude parks show one. If Altitude doesn't name it for Tampa, I did not count it.

Their safety rules say watch a short orientation video and sign the waiver before you jump. Book the Park is offered at participating locations. Nobody told me whether this park is one of them. I am not promising a private rental of the whole building.

The prices Altitude puts out are a pile, and I am only repeating numbers they actually gave, with the warning that they do not all look current. In one cluster: 120-Minute Jump $27.99, Happy Hour 3 Hr Jump Monday through Thursday $27.99, 90-Minute Jump $24.99, and monthly memberships for $44.99 that say come in and ask. A $100 deposit is required for event bookings. Separate party prices: a 5 jumper package starting at $189.99 plus tax, $25.99 per additional jumper, 10 jumper packages starting at $229.99 plus tax, and private rentals starting at $899.99 facility fee plus jump time. Other prices they still had out are older or dated: an Easter egg hunt for 3/29/24 at $21.99 pre-sale and $24.99 day of, and a flash sale "until 4/17/26" for one hour at $9.99. Today is October 4, 2026. That April date has passed, and the 2024 egg hunt is not a ticket. I would not walk up quoting $9.99 or $24.99 from an old promo. I would check current passes with them, or call one of the two numbers.

Sky Zone is a different Tampa park, not a second Altitude

The other business the generic phrase keeps pointing to is Sky Zone Tampa. Sky Zone gives 10137 E Adamo Dr, Ste 800B, Tampa, FL 33619, phone (813) 833-1740. That is east, not West Hillsborough, and it is still inside the city of Tampa. I am not folding it into Altitude and I am not pretending it does not exist. Their waiver asks you to confirm you mean Tampa, FL, because the chain has a long list of other parks.

Sky Zone's hours: Monday through Thursday 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Attractions they name for Tampa: iWall, Freestyle Jump, SkySlam, Ultimate Dodgeball, Foam Zone, Speed Zone, Boulder Balls, Sky Tower, FreeClimb, Ninja Warrior Course, Parkour Blox, and Air Court. They say every Sky Zone is unique, so I am treating that list as this location's labels, not as a promise copied from another city.

I never got a general-admission dollar figure from Sky Zone Tampa. What they did offer were promos. Buy 2 tickets, get 2 free, plus 20 percent off food and drinks, and they say you can use them at once or save some. A weekday birthday deal uses code BDAY25 for 25 percent off a Mega or Epic party hosted Monday through Friday, minimum 10 jumpers, terms apply. Percentages are not a walk-up jump price. I am not inventing one. Memberships, group rates, and renting the park are offered as products, without a number I am willing to repeat.

When you choose a park, Sky Zone also offers Clearwater, Bradenton - Lakewood, Lakeland, and Sarasota as their own Sky Zone names. Those are not the Adamo Drive park. If the waiver is asking which park, Tampa is the one with the Adamo address above. Clearwater is a different selection.

Altitude Bradenton and Altitude Spring Hill are separate Altitude parks. I am not giving them this Hillsborough address, and I am not calling either one Tampa because a map said near.

How I would use the phrase

If someone says tampa trampoline place, I ask which brand. Altitude at 4340 West Hillsborough, suite question included, is one answer. Sky Zone at 10137 E Adamo Drive, suite 800B, is the other answer that is actually in Tampa. They do not share hours, phones, or attraction lists. Lutz and New Port Richey are a drive to a different city. Anyone who also mentions Rays tickets, a Bucs stadium, Lightning, a Seminole Hard Rock casino, an AMC, a hotel, or a show is just describing the rest of a Tampa weekend. None of those is a jump pass. I would sign the waiver before I leave the house, and I would not quote a price off a 2024 egg hunt or an April 2026 flash sale that is still floating around.
