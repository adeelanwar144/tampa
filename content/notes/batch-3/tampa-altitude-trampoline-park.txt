People say tampa altitude trampoline park, and the flipped version, altitude trampoline park tampa, and I wanted to find out how many of these parks they mean when they say Tampa. I did not want a second address I had talked myself into. Altitude's own list of locations is the only thing I was willing to trust.

Altitude files exactly one park under Tampa: Altitude Tampa, Drew Park. Drew Park is the neighborhood label in that name. It is not a second park. Altitude gives the address as 4340 West Hillsborough Avenue, Tampa, Florida 33614, and in another place writes it as 4340 West Hillsborough Ave Suite 350, Tampa, Florida 33614. The street and ZIP match. The suite does not appear in the shorter version. I would put Suite 350 in the pin and still look for the door, because Altitude itself is not consistent about whether the suite is part of the public address.

The same location shows two phone numbers, (813) 399-1529 and (813) 503-4428. Nothing told me which one is the front desk and which one is something else. I am writing both down and not guessing.

The hours Altitude gives for Tampa are more specific than a plain "Hours" heading. Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Friday 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Saturday 9:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. I am treating that as what Tampa posted when I asked, not as a promise that a holiday week matches. I did not find a second official set of hours that contradicted those times the way the skatepark's posted hours contradicted each other. If the door says something else, the door wins. I just did not see a second published set.

What Altitude says is actually in the Tampa building: Basketball Trampoline Dunk, Battle Beam, Extreme Dodgeball, Kid's Court, Main Court, Performance Trampoline, Trapeze, Tumble Track, and ZipLine. The programs named for Tampa are Birthday Party On, Glo in the Park, Jump Happy Hour, and Junior Jumpers Jam. Altitude's broader attractions material is less useful than that list. Much of it is a generic "attractions may differ from park to park" warning, and the photos mixed in come from other cities. I am not padding the Tampa park with a foam pit, a ninja run, or a climbing wall just because those words showed up in a shared gallery. If they are not on the Tampa list, I did not count them.

Altitude's safety instructions say to watch a short orientation video and sign the waiver ahead of time. I would do that before driving over. A trampoline park that turns you around at the desk for a missing waiver is a wasted trip, and Altitude is already telling you the waiver exists.

Prices for Tampa, which I am copying only because Altitude posted them and not because I paid them: a 90-minute jump at $24.99, a 120-minute jump at $27.99, a Happy Hour 3 hour jump at $27.99 on Monday through Thursday, and monthly memberships for $44.99 with a note to come in and ask. For events, Altitude says a $100 deposit is required, and that if you book ahead without coming in, someone from Altitude will call to collect it, so the contact info has to be real. One promo I saw offered a Monday through Thursday deal with a code. I am not treating a code price as the walk-up price. Jump time, happy hour, membership, and deposit are four different numbers for four different things. None of them is a parking fee, a sock fee, or a party package total. Altitude says party packages and room options vary by location, and that booking the whole park is only at participating locations. I could not tell whether this specific park is one of the participating ones. I am not saying you can rent the building, and I am not saying you cannot.

The Florida list is how I answered the "is there another Tampa" question. Altitude files parks under these city names: Bradenton, Coral Springs, Jacksonville Beach, Kissimmee, Sanford, Spring Hill, Tampa, and West Palm Beach. Tampa appears once. There is no Brandon park, no Clearwater park, no St. Petersburg park, and no second Hillsborough Avenue park. I looked into the two that a Tampa visitor could reasonably confuse with "the Tampa one" because they are closer than Miami.

Altitude Bradenton is called Bradenton - Sarasota. The address is 201 Cortez Road West, Bradenton, Florida 34207. The phone is different from both Tampa numbers. The attraction list is also different: AeroStrike, Battle Beam, DodgeAttack, Dodgeball, FreeStyle Run, Freedom Gaming, High Nine, Main Court, Selfie Cam, Sport Court Basketball, Stunt Jump 9ft, Tumble Track, and ValoJump. That is not the Tampa list. No trapeze, no zip line, and several names Tampa does not use. If someone tells you Sarasota or Bradenton, it is this park, not Drew Park.

Altitude Spring Hill is 11162 Spring Hill Drive, Spring Hill, Florida 34609, again with its own phone. Its list is AeroStrike, Battle Beam, Extreme Dodgeball, High Nine, Main Court, Stunt Jump 6ft, Trapeze, ValoJump, and Trapeze named a second time. Spring Hill is not Tampa. The stunt jump there is labeled 6ft. Bradenton's is labeled 9ft. Tampa's list does not use that stunt-jump name at all. Three parks, three inventories. I am not going to call Spring Hill or Bradenton a Tampa location because a map app decided they were "near" me.

The farther Florida parks, Coral Springs, Jacksonville Beach, Kissimmee, Sanford, and West Palm Beach, are on the same list and even less relevant to a Tampa trip. I did not look into each one. I only needed to see that none of them is filed as Tampa. One Tampa park is the finding.

People asking about this alongside Rays tickets, a Bucs stadium, Lightning, a Seminole casino, an AMC, a hotel, or a concert are in the wrong building. This is a trampoline park on West Hillsborough in Drew Park, suite question and all. Altitude gives one Tampa address, one set of hours, a short attraction list, and two phone numbers it does not explain. I would call one of those numbers before I promised a kid a specific jump price, because the dollar figures I saw were mixed in with promo language, and I would not drive to Bradenton or Spring Hill thinking either sign said Tampa.
