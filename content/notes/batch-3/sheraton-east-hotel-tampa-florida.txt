Notes on sheraton east hotel tampa florida

People still ask for the sheraton east hotel tampa florida, and because I already had a note on the Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel, I did not want to assume they were the same building. The name people use has no Brandon in it. It has East. I needed the old name and the current name to land on the same street, or I was going to treat them as two Sheratons and send someone to the wrong door.

They are the same hotel. The name changed. The East name did not fully die.

The old name, from the 2012 debut

Starwood announced on July 27, 2012 that the Sheraton Tampa East Hotel had debuted across the street from Sabal Park Business Center. It was a former Crowne Plaza. They described a 9 million dollar renovation before the Sheraton flag went up, and they called it the brand's fourth hotel in greater Tampa. Starwood counted 265 guest rooms, all renovated, with the Sheraton Sweet Sleeper bed, an oversized work desk, an ergonomic chair, and a flat-panel television. The restaurant in that announcement is spelled Panfilio's Bar and Grille. Meeting space was more than 30,000 square feet, including a 5,000 square foot ballroom, plus a poolside pavilion. Amenities they named: Sheraton Club Lounge, an outdoor heated pool, and a fitness facility. The location as Starwood put it: off I-75, within five miles of the Tampa Convention Center and the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel and Casino, just ten miles from downtown Tampa and 16 miles from Tampa International Airport, with Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Brandon Town Center, and Ford Amphitheater in the same breath.

That is the East hotel people are still asking for. It was also already talking about Brandon Town Center in 2012. The East name and the Brandon geography were overlapping on day one. I am not treating that 9 million dollar figure as a current rate. It is a renovation number from a 2012 announcement.

What Marriott calls it now

Marriott now calls it the Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel. Marriott's hotel code for it is still TPAEA. I read that EA as East that nobody bothered to retire. I could not get Marriott's general overview of the hotel, but I did get the room details, and I had already seen dining and experiences under the Brandon name.

The address Marriott gives: 10221 Princess Palm Avenue, Tampa, Florida, USA, 33610. Main phone +1 813-623-6363. Toll free +1-800-352-3535. Fax +1 813-621-7224. They advertise it as fully renovated. Pets up to 75 pounds with a small fee. The fee is not stated. I am not filling one in, and I am not writing a nightly room rate. Marriott described balconies, pillowtop beds, robes, a penthouse with a dining area and a bar, and a Sheraton Club lounge. I was not given a price.

Club hours, which I had not written down before: general hours Monday to Friday 5:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., Saturday and Sunday closed. Breakfast Monday to Friday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m., Saturday and Sunday 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. Beer and wine carry a fee. Complimentary for Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members plus one guest. So the lounge is not a seven-day club someone can wander into on a Saturday evening. At the time I asked, Marriott was also showing no accessible rooms available at this hotel, with a note that there may be a limited number and to select one while booking. I am reading that as inventory at that moment, not as a permanent claim that the building has none.

Marriott still says one outdoor pool for guests, and a complimentary fitness center. It does not, in what I was told, repeat the 2012 word heated. I am not merging "heated" from 2012 into today's description.

Where the East name survives

Visit Tampa Bay has the hotel as Sheraton Tampa Brandon, at the same street: 10221 Princess Palm Ave, Tampa, FL 33610, phone (813) 623-6363. They describe it as off I-75 and slightly south of I-4 in Sabal Park Business Center, and then "among hotels in Brandon, Florida." So the postal city is Tampa, the pitch is Brandon, and the old name is East. That is three labels for one driveway.

Visit Tampa Bay says 266 newly renovated guest rooms and suites, king or two double Sweet Sleeper beds. Starwood said 265 in 2012. I am not rounding them into one number. Visit Tampa Bay gives check-in at 4 p.m., check-out 11 a.m., cancellation 48 hours prior to arrival. Five floors, three elevators, 105 double/double, 119 kings, 41 suites, 10 handicap accessible rooms, 21 meeting rooms. Largest meeting room 5,005 square feet. Total meeting rooms 28,254 square feet. Miles from Tampa International: 16. Miles to the Tampa Convention Center: 8. The 16 miles matches the 2012 airport figure. The convention-center figure does not match the 2012 "within five miles," and in 2012 Starwood also said just ten miles from downtown. I am leaving those as two descriptions that disagree, not as a distance I measured.

Other details Visit Tampa Bay gives that I am attributing only to them: a free scheduled shuttle to shops, restaurants, and attractions within three miles, naming Seminole Hard Rock Casino, Westfield Brandon Mall, Mid Florida Credit Union Amphitheatre, and the Florida State Fairgrounds. Free parking. A lap pool. Basketball and tennis courts. 410 parking spaces and 3 motorcoach spaces. AAA three diamond. Marriott never told me parking was free, so if the desk quotes a fee, the desk wins over the visitor bureau.

Then the restaurant, which is the proof I wanted. Panfilo's Bar & Grill, same address, 10221 Princess Palm Ave, same phone (813) 623-6363. Visit Tampa Bay still describes Panfilo's as located in the Sheraton Tampa East. The restaurant's entry was never updated off the East name. Hours they give: Monday to Thursday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. Sunday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Live entertainment Monday to Friday 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. Indoor seats 104. Visit Tampa Bay's food-and-beverage description for the hotel itself adds a lunch block, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m., which the Panfilo's hours do not show, and it says the lobby bar serves food until 10 p.m. with the room open until midnight on Friday and Saturday. I am not going to pretend those two descriptions run on one clock. The East restaurant entry and the Brandon hotel entry have drifted.

Spelling drifted too. 2012 says Panfilio's Bar and Grille. Today it is Panfilo's Bar & Grill. Close enough that I believe it is the same restaurant, not a second one down the street. Not close enough that I would only ever ask for one spelling.

What I would not do with this name

I would not book a second hotel because the name says East and the sign says Brandon. The street is Princess Palm, the ZIP Marriott gives is 33610, and the phone 813-623-6363 belongs to both the current hotel and the old East restaurant entry. East was the brand name for this Sabal Park property. Brandon is the name they use now, even while the mailing city stays Tampa, Florida, USA.

I would not trust the 2012 distances against the current figures without asking. Five miles versus eight miles to the convention center is not a rounding error I am willing to smooth over. Sixteen miles to the airport is the figure both the 2012 announcement and Visit Tampa Bay give, and it is still not a shuttle promise. The shuttle Visit Tampa Bay describes is within three miles, to the casino, the mall, the amphitheatre, and the fairgrounds. That is not an airport van. I already separated those ideas in the Brandon note. I am only repeating the distinction because the East name makes people think they found a different, closer hotel.

I would not invent a rate. Nobody gave me a nightly number I am willing to write down. The small pet fee is unnamed. Club beer and wine carry a fee and nobody said how much.

The difficulty is the name. Sheraton east hotel tampa florida is a 2012 name that a restaurant entry never updated, sitting on top of Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel at 10221 Princess Palm Avenue. One building. Postal city Tampa. Pitch says Brandon. People still say East. If I only remembered one of those three, I would think I had the wrong Sheraton.
