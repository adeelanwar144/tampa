Notes on shopping centers in tampa florida

I went looking for shopping centers in Tampa Florida, and people also say the shorter shopping center tampa fl in the same breath. I did not want a ranked list. I wrote down the centers I actually looked into and what each one says about itself. If I did not look into a name, the name is not in this note. Nothing here is an order of which one is better.

Which addresses said Tampa, and which did not

Four of the centers give a Tampa street address. Two do not, and that was the first problem. International Plaza and Bay Street is 2223 N. Westshore Boulevard, Tampa, FL 33607. WestShore Plaza is 250 WestShore Plaza, Tampa, FL 33609. Hyde Park Village is 1602 W. Snow Ave, Tampa, FL 33606. Citrus Park Town Center is 8021 Citrus Park Town Center, Tampa, FL 33625. Those are the four I am willing to call Tampa shopping centers by their own addresses.

Tampa Premium Outlets is 2300 Grand Cypress Drive, Lutz, FL 33559. The outlets describe themselves as in Wesley Chapel near Lutz, Pasco County, off Interstate 75 and State Road 56, just 20 miles north of Tampa International Airport. The Shops at Wiregrass is 28211 Paseo Drive, Suite 100, Wesley Chapel, FL 33543. I am keeping both because people asking about Tampa get pointed to them. They are not the same errand as Westshore.

International Plaza and Bay Street

The operator calls it International Plaza and Bay Street, and also calls it a shopping center. I had been saying mall. The address is the 33607 one above. Mall management is (813) 342-3780. The shopping line is (813) 342-3790. Security is (813) 342-3789.

The center says more than 170 stores and 30 places to eat, in the Westshore district, minutes from the airport, with Bay Street outside. Visit Tampa Bay, for the same address, says 170 stores and 16 restaurants. I am not averaging those. The store count is not settled.

Regular hours are Monday through Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. and Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The posted hours showed Sunday, October 4, 2026, as 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Monday, October 5, as 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026, closed, and Christmas Day, December 25, 2026, closed. Visit Tampa Bay adds that department store and restaurant hours vary. I am reading the posted hours as the corridor, not as Nordstrom or a Bay Street kitchen.

The luxury names the center leads with are Neiman Marcus, Nordstrom, Gucci, Tiffany & Co., Saint Laurent, Burberry, Versace, Chanel beauty, and a run of watch brands including Breitling, IWC, Omega, and TAG Heuer. That is the glitz they advertise. I did not see those brands ranked, and I am not ranking them.

Parking is three garages, one at Dillard's and Neiman Marcus, one at Nordstrom, and one under Bay Street, plus paid parking near the entrances. They spell that paid parking "up-font," which I am reading as up-front, and they do not give a dollar amount for it. Valet is the part with a price: Monday through Thursday $10 a car, Friday through Sunday $12 a car, all day, and complimentary if you are in a Lexus. I am copying those figures only because the center posts them.

The Renaissance Tampa International Plaza Hotel is next to this center. I am not filing the hotel as a shopping center. I am only marking it because people asking about the plaza, the mall, and the hotel all end up on this same Westshore block, and it is easy to confuse the hotel with the mall.

WestShore Plaza

This is the other one. 250 WestShore Plaza, Tampa, FL 33609, main phone (813) 286-0790. The mall management office is (813) 286-0792. I was never given posted center hours. I am not inventing a closing time.

The store directory is what told me this is not a second luxury plaza. Names in it included JCPenney, AMC WestShore 14, H&M, Old Navy, Maggiano's Little Italy, P.F. Chang's, Seasons 52, and Arden Bridal & Boutique, plus Airport Parking by Safeway. A theater, a bridal shop, and a department store. The zip is 33609, south of the 33607 plaza. A pin that only says Westshore is not specific enough.

Hyde Park Village

Not an enclosed mall. The village calls itself a six-block haven of boutiques, brands, and food, with fountains, the Fresh Market, and a boutique hotel it does not name. I noted LoveShackFancy marked as newly arrived, Ralph Lauren, and Veronica Beard. Address 1602 W. Snow Ave, Tampa, FL 33606, phone (813) 251-3500. The village says the neighborhood was once citrus groves.

The hours fight each other. One set of posted hours says Monday through Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. and Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Another set says Monday through Saturday the same, and Sunday 12:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. Both say to check the shop itself. I would not guess the Sunday start. Parking in designated garages and surface lots is complimentary. On-street parking and valet cost a small fee, and no number is posted, so I have no number. Outdoor space and patios are pet-friendly. That is a different kind of afternoon than a food court.

Citrus Park Town Center

8021 Citrus Park Town Center, Tampa, FL 33625, phone (813) 926-4644. Hours, posted twice and the same both times: Monday through Thursday 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. A Monday plan that works at International Plaza at 10:00 a.m. does not work here. The doors on that schedule are not open yet.

The directory names Dillard's, JCPenney, Macy's, and Dick's Sporting Goods, along with H&M. Glam Korean Skincare is one of the names in the accessories group. I wrote it down because I had the word glam in my notes and this was the store that actually used it. NCG Cinema and Kids Empire are in the same directory, so the building is not only shops. The center warns that some former retail spaces were converted to offices, storage, and similar uses, or demolished, and are no longer counted, and that the store list can change. I am not treating every name as a shop that is still selling. Foot Locker was marked as newly arrived. A Halloween gift-card promo was running. I did not enter it. The center's information was current.

The outlets, which are not a Tampa address

Tampa Premium Outlets is open-air, at the Lutz address above. Management (813) 909-8716, shopping line (813) 938-6272, security (813) 545-5615. Regular hours are Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Sunday 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The outlets name Ralph Lauren, Vineyard Vines, Brooks Brothers, Marc Jacobs, Coach Outlet, Lacoste, Adidas, Nike Factory Store, Ulta Beauty, and OFFLINE by Aerie. For food they name The Cheesecake Factory, which they say debuted in late 2025, plus BJ's Restaurant and Brewhouse, LongHorn Steakhouse, and in Market Hall Charleys Cheesesteaks, Poke Lulu, Tacos El Patron, and McDonald's.

Up Front parking is $7 for the whole visit, at the main entrance by Colombia, adidas, and Guess. That price is posted. Wheelchairs are at the management office. Ride-share pickup was marked coming soon. The bus is Pasco County Route 54. From the airport, the outlets' directions are I-275 north, then I-75 north, Exit 275, left onto State Road 56. I would not staple this onto a Hyde Park afternoon.

Wiregrass

The Shops at Wiregrass say very little about themselves. Address and phone (813) 994-2242. They describe retail, dining, and entertainment together, and they name Bosphorus Turkish Cuisine as new. Sunday, October 4, 2026, was posted as 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. The hours for one tenant, 365 Caffe Italiano, did not match, including a Friday and Saturday close that read 9:30 a.m. I am not using that. I never got a weekly schedule I trusted, so I am not writing Monday hours.

Their directions put a Pasco Route 54 stop at SR 56 and Wiregrass Mall, and a weekday ride from the airport that changes on weekends. A Tampa shopping trip that ends in a two-county bus ride is how someone misses the last bus.

What I thought people were struggling with

Shopping center tampa fl is not one building. Of the ones I looked into, it is four Tampa addresses plus two centers in Lutz and Wesley Chapel. International Plaza is the luxury indoor center with Bay Street outside and a hotel on the same block. WestShore Plaza is the other Westshore property, with JCPenney and the theater, and I still do not have its hours. Hyde Park is six blocks of boutiques, and its two sets of posted hours do not agree on Sunday. Citrus Park starts later on weekdays, and it warns that some old shop spaces are gone. The outlets are the 20-mile drive. Wiregrass is Wesley Chapel. I am not calling any of them the best one. I am only keeping the address each center itself gives.
