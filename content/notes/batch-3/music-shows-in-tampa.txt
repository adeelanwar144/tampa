Notes on music shows in tampa

I put this together on Sunday, October 4, 2026. I am writing the date in the first line because a concert list goes stale as soon as a show plays or a venue stops updating its schedule. Anything I name here is something a venue actually had on its schedule when I checked. I am not filling a blank with a lineup from memory.

There is no one calendar

"Music shows in Tampa" is not a venue. It is several rooms that do not share a schedule. Visit Tampa Bay's events pitch is general, entertainment, festivals, food, and culture, downtown through Ybor. It names no artist. I could not get a current Straz lineup. If you are mid-purchase or need help, the Straz ticket sales office is (813) 229-7827, Tuesday through Sunday 12 to 7 p.m. and 90 minutes before a show. I am not inventing a Straz bill. The amphitheater would not give me titles either. The useful finding is the venue calendars that actually name someone, and even those are a snapshot. October 3 is already yesterday.

Raymond James Stadium is a football stadium that sometimes books tours

A lot of the stadium's dates are kickoff times, a Bulls stampede, or "tbd," with no artist. The policies repeat: no-bag rule, one clutch no larger than 4.5 by 6.5 inches, mobile tickets only, no screenshots, ticket office only on event days, suite questions at (813) 998-3877. The kids' rule is not stable. For one event, ages three and under are free if they do not take a seat. For a Buccaneers-style event, every patron needs a ticket. For a concert-style event, ages two and under do not need a ticket if they do not take a seat. I would not carry last week's rule onto this week's show.

One show I got cleanly. Clara La San, Chosen Silences Tour Fall 2026, Thursday, October 29, 2026, at The Ritz Ybor. Billed as 7:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m., and also as doors 7 p.m., show 8 p.m. Address: 1503 E 7th Ave, Tampa, FL 33605. General admission, standing room, no personal chairs. Cashless. What I would do next time

Pick the building, then the artist. Channelside, Franklin Street, Ybor, USF, the stadium, and 301 are not one district. Check whether the date is hockey, a kickoff, a film, or a band. Ignore "tonight." Ignore a schedule that will not name anyone. And check the date on the note. Mine says Sunday, October 4, 2026. By next weekend this list is already a little bit of a lie.
