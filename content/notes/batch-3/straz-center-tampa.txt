Notes on straz center tampa

I use the short version on purpose: straz center tampa. Not the full David A. Straz name, not "performing arts center," not the old TBPAC initials. That is what people actually say when a ticket or a friend says "meet me at the Straz." I wanted to know what that short name still pointed at on Sunday, October 4, 2026, and whether the center still treated Straz Center and the Tampa Bay Performing Arts Center as the same downtown campus.

Same campus, shorter label

The center brands itself Straz Center. The logo, the ticket pitches, and the Boundless campaign all say Straz. Its audited financial statements still carry the longer legal story: Tampa Bay Performing Arts Center, Inc. and affiliate, doing business as the David A. Straz, Jr. Center for the Performing Arts. The corporation formed September 15, 1980, to run the facility under a lease with the City of Tampa. In November 2009 an endowment gift went to the foundation, and for that gift the center was renamed for branding. It began doing business as the David A. Straz, Jr. Center for the Performing Arts effective November 11, 2009. The building did not move. The short name and the long legal name land on the same riverfront campus.

That is the confusion I kept running into. Someone asking about "straz center tampa" often already has a show night in mind. Someone using the old initials is usually trying to confirm the rename. Both should end at 1010 N. WC MacInnes Place, Tampa, Florida 33602. The center's rental policies give that street address for the administrative office and the ticket office. They also drop the Jr. in places and spell MacInnes two ways in the same document. I would copy the payee off whatever instructions come with the ticket or the rental, not invent a spelling.

What was actually on sale

When I looked, the center was selling tickets, not sitting closed. Upcoming titles: The Sound of Music, Buena Vista Social Club, Black Violin, Misery, After Dark, and Hamilton. The 26/27 Bank of America Broadway season was on sale as a build-your-own package, with the ticket sales office at (813) 229-7827 for details. Patel Conservatory classes for the 26/27 school year were on sale. Opera Tampa's 2027 season of three Italian works was on sale. A Premium Experience Lounge add-on was offered for select performances in Morsani Hall. I am not inventing prices for any of those. I am only naming what the center was pushing when I looked.

Boundless, and whether the doors stay open

At the same time, the center was fundraising for BOUNDLESS: the campaign for the new Straz. In late October 2025, the Straz said the multimillion-dollar renovation includes expanding education spaces for Patel Conservatory, refurbished lobbies for Morsani and Ferguson halls, an indoor-outdoor terrace, a fine-dining restaurant, a rooftop bar, and a Riverwalk-facing outdoor stage. Ground broke April 7. Completion was aimed at 2027. Chief Operating Officer Matt Wolf said roads stay the same, Straz remains fully functional, valet still runs, and Maestro's restaurant, the grab-n-go café, and SteamHeat Coffee Shop stay open. The short answer from the center, twice, was that shows go on during construction. That matches what I saw: construction talk and ticket sales sitting next to each other.

I thought people were struggling with the idea that "the new Straz" meant a second address. From everything the center said, Boundless is an expansion of this campus, not a move across town.

Parking is the hard part of the short name

When I tried to sort out the visit details, I was told to call the ticket sales office at (813) 229-7827, Tuesday through Sunday noon to 7 p.m. and 90 minutes before a show. The parking guidance is what I would actually use. The center tells you to pick a lot or garage before show day, use pre-paid parking when it is offered, and put the garage address in the GPS, not "Straz Center," unless you are using valet. Drop-off splits by room: Fortune Street for the Patel Conservatory; Fortune Street or the Arrival Plaza for Morsani Hall; the Arrival Plaza for Ferguson Hall; the corner of MacInnes Place and Tyler Street for the Jaeb Theater or the Shimberg Playhouse. The Arrival Plaza sits on Tyler Street and is limited to ticket purchases, meetings, valet when available, pickups and drop-offs, and disabled parking. It is not the main event garage.

Valet through Metropolis in that Arrival Plaza was 40 dollars per vehicle for regular and ADA valet, plus a 5 percent service fee, credit card only. When full valet is not available, event self-parking there was 30 dollars including ADA. Those are the center's own figures, not a guess at a downtown meter. Anchor Garage at 109 W. Fortune Street has 319 spaces the center says it secured for guests Monday through Friday after 6 p.m. and all day Saturday and Sunday. Ashley Plaza Garage at 110 W. Fortune Street has 200 on the same pattern. Rates vary with other downtown events. Poe Garage at 800 N. Ashley Drive wants you to pay by license plate at a pay station before the show. Rideshare pickup, they say, belongs on the north side near Fortune Street. Pirate Water Taxi Stop 14 is in front of the building.

Hall names still matter

Even the short name drops you into a campus with more than one door. The rental policies describe Carol Morsani Hall and Louise Lykes Ferguson Hall as the halls with ticket windows outside them. The drop-off guidance also names the Jaeb and the Shimberg. If your friend says "the Straz" and your ticket says Ferguson, those are the same campus and different curbs. I would ask which hall is printed on the ticket before I trust a single map pin.

Tickets and the phone people actually dial

The ticket sales number that keeps coming up is (813) 229-7827, also given as 813-229-STAR. The rental policies also list 800-955-1045 for the ticket office and 813-222-1000 for the administrative office. Rentals programming is a different desk: Jeanne Piazza at 813-222-1017 on the staff list in that same packet. "Straz Center" as a Broadway seat and "Straz Center" as a room rental are not the same phone tree. The rental packet says a date hold lasts 30 days without a contract, then releases. I am noting that because the short name feels like a ticket question, and then someone tries to book a wedding through the same people.

What this name is not

Saying straz center tampa next to Rays tickets, a Bucs stadium night, Lightning at the arena, Seminole Hard Rock on Orient Road, an AMC, or a hotel does not put those venues on MacInnes Place. Seminole Hard Rock gives its address as 5223 Orient Road, Tampa, Florida 33610. That is a hotel and casino entertainment complex, not Carol Morsani Hall. Raymond James Stadium and Benchmark International Arena are the big rock and sports rooms. Straz is the downtown performing arts campus with Broadway, opera, and the conservatory classes sharing an address.

What I am keeping from the short name: Straz Center is the brand name on the tickets. The legal corporation still carries Tampa Bay Performing Arts Center. The November 11, 2009 rename is branding after an endowment, not a new building. Shows were on sale while Boundless construction aimed at 2027. The center's parking guidance is the useful part, and it punishes you for navigating to the MacInnes Place pin instead of a garage. Call 229-STAR if anything else fails. Ask which hall before you trust the curb.
