Notes on renaissance tampa international plaza hotel

I went through everything Marriott and the hotel put out for the Renaissance Tampa International Plaza Hotel: the overview, the rooms, the dining, the things-to-do pitch, the meetings contact, and two hotel documents, a welcome letter and a hotel information sheet. I never got a nightly room rate. I am not inventing one. The hotel does give other charges, and I am keeping those separate from a rate I did not see.

The name and the door

The hotel's own name is Renaissance Tampa International Plaza Hotel, sometimes written Renaissance Tampa International Plaza hotel, lowercase h. Address every time the hotel gives it: 4200 Jim Walter Boulevard, Tampa, Florida 33607. Phone (813) 877-9200. Toll free +1-800-468-3571. Fax +1 813-877-3000. The hotel calls itself a 4-star hotel. That is their label, not my score.

It sits adjacent to International Plaza Mall and Bay Street, in the Westshore Business District. The hotel says it is a short drive from Ybor City. It also says just 3 miles from Tampa International Airport, and gives the drive: Airport Service Road, first exit at the traffic circle, left on North Westshore Boulevard, onto Jim Walter Boulevard, then right to stay on it. I would follow the circle.

I was given 1 mile from the airport for the mall. The hotel says 3 miles for itself. I am not forcing those into one distance. The mall also says adjacent. I would not walk from baggage claim.

Check-in, from the welcome letter

Check-in 4:00 p.m. Check-out 12:00 p.m. Minimum check-in age 18. Pets: service animals only. There is no pet fee because the letter does not allow pets. Cancellation per the letter: no charge if you cancel 5 days before arrival, by 11:59 p.m. hotel time. I would read my own reservation anyway. The letter is a reminder, not my booking.

When I checked, the hotel had no accessible rooms available. That can change by the hour. Room sizes, the hotel says, are approximate. I could not get answers on a workspace, coffee maker, hair dryer, rollaways, or an in-room hot tub. I am not filling those in. The hotel says guestrooms and suites have 55-inch smart TVs, Wi-Fi, and mini-refrigerators. Club-level rooms include breakfast, appetizers, and a concierge lounge.

The hotel pushes a day pass for the pool and fitness center, or a day room, for a long layover. No price was attached. I do not have a day-room rate.

Parking, internet, and the charger

These numbers are in the welcome letter and the hotel information sheet, not on a room-rate grid.

Self-parking is $15 overnight. Valet is $35 overnight and $15 for the day. Valet hours are Sunday through Thursday 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 7:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. That valet is the hotel's. The mall next door runs its own valet, cheaper and on a different schedule. I am not mixing the two bills.

Electric car charging, per the hotel information sheet: first 4 hours complimentary, each additional hour $5, energy rate $0.39 per kWh.

Internet, per the welcome letter: complimentary high-speed for Marriott Bonvoy members. If you are not a member, standard internet is $12.95 a day and high-speed is $16.95 a day. I would join before I paid that, but I am not treating the join pitch as a discount on the room. There was no room price to discount.

Airport shuttle

Both the welcome letter and the hotel information sheet say the airport shuttle runs 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. daily, and that you must call (813) 877-9200 to confirm pickup. The information sheet names the zones: Red 1, Red 2, Blue 1, Blue 2, and the airport rental car area. I would not stand at a random curb. The mall's own list of hotel shuttles does not include this Renaissance. This shuttle is the hotel's own run to Tampa International Airport. It is not a shopping shuttle, and it is not 24 hours. A flight outside that window is a car.

Food in the building

Gabriella's is the restaurant. The hotel calls it Mediterranean, Spanish small plates, breakfast and then cocktails, casual dress, and the phone is the hotel phone. Hours, per the hotel's dining information: Monday through Thursday and Sunday, 6:30 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. and 11:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday, those same spans plus 10:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m. The welcome letter says Gabriella's is open Monday through Sunday 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., with breakfast 6:30 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., and it uses the phrase Tampa International Mall for the shopping beforehand. I am not sure whether the kitchen opens at 6:00 or 6:30, or whether Friday night really runs to 11:00. Two Marriott documents, two answers. I would call.

Breakfast is available at Gabriella's, in-room dining, and The Market, and the hotel says for a fee. No breakfast price is given. I am not guessing.

In-room dining runs every day 6:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., Mediterranean, and the hotel confirms room service exists. The Market is American, open 24 hours, at the front desk, salads, sandwiches, drinks, and snacks, for the mini-fridge or for eating now.

The hotel's dining information also names California Pizza Kitchen, (813) 353-8155, lunch and dinner. That is a mall restaurant, not a second hotel kitchen. The hotel also gave another lunch-and-dinner number without a name attached. I am not assigning it.

The welcome letter describes an evening ritual, complimentary, Monday through Thursday, 5:00 to 5:30 p.m. I would not show up at 5:40 and expect it.

Concierge lounge

The hotel puts the Concierge Club Lounge on the 8th floor, for qualifying members or as an upgrade. Complimentary for Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite, plus one guest. The hotel information sheet has the hours I trust more, because it includes Friday: Monday through Thursday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Friday 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., Saturday closed, Sunday 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. The welcome letter says Monday through Thursday 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Saturday closed, Sunday 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m., and it skips Friday in that sentence. It also says breakfast Monday through Friday 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., appetizers Sunday through Thursday 5:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m., and dessert Sunday through Thursday 7:30 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. Saturday closed means I would not book a club room for a Saturday stay and expect that lounge. Friday looks like breakfast only, if I follow the information sheet.

Pool and fitness

Fitness center, per the hotel overview and the welcome letter: open 7 days, 24 hours, complimentary for guests. Equipment named includes cardio machines, free weights, and strength machines. I am not adding a class schedule nobody gave.

The pool conflicts. The hotel overview says the outdoor pool is Monday through Sunday 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., heated, towels provided, complimentary, with a whirlpool near the pool deck, also complimentary, and no separate whirlpool hours. The welcome letter says the outdoor heated pool is 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Same problem as the restaurant start time. I know it is outside, heated, and closed overnight. I do not know if the gate is at 6:00 or 7:00. The hotel also mentions an outdoor patio or courtyard. I am not describing a view I did not see.

Other house rules from the letter

Laundry drops at the front desk between 6:00 a.m. and 8:30 a.m., back the same day after 7:00 p.m. I would miss that window if I slept in.

The welcome letter says Bonvoy members can use the app for mobile check-in, mobile key, and mobile dining. I am not claiming the key works every night.

What the hotel wants me to do in town

The hotel's things-to-do pitch is a brochure. International Plaza and Bay Street are the shopping next door. Raymond James Stadium and George M. Steinbrenner Field are less than 2 miles from the hotel, by their count. It also names ZooTampa, the Florida Aquarium, Busch Gardens, the Riverwalk, and Ybor, including The Columbia Restaurant, which it calls the oldest restaurant in Florida. I am not inventing drive times for those. The hotel only says it is near them, except for that under-2-miles figure and the airport's 3 miles.

What I thought people were struggling with

The name is long, and every word in it also belongs to something else. International is the plaza and the airport. Plaza is the mall. Renaissance is the hotel. People ask for the renaissance tampa international plaza hotel and then try to use the mall's valet price, or the mall's hours, or a room rate nobody ever gave them. The hotel's own documents disagree with each other on Gabriella's start time, the pool's opening time, and whether Friday exists at the club lounge. No accessible rooms were available when I checked. The shuttle stops at 11:00 p.m. and you have to call for a colored zone. Self-parking is $15 overnight and valet overnight is $35, and neither number is a room rate. I still do not know what a night costs. I would book through the hotel itself, not from this note, and I would call (813) 877-9200 before I counted on breakfast at 6:00 a.m. or the club lounge on Saturday.
