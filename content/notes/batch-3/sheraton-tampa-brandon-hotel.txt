Notes on the Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel

People ask for the sheraton tampa brandon hotel, and the tighter version is sheraton tampa brandon hotel tampa fl. Both point at the same property. Marriott's code for it is TPAEA. I never got Marriott's general overview of the hotel. What I did get from Marriott were the room details, the dining, and the experiences around it. I also went to AAA's description of the same hotel, because I wanted a second published description, not a rate I made up.

Name versus the city on the address

Every time Marriott gave me an address it was the same: 10221 Princess Palm Avenue, Tampa, Florida, USA, 33610. Toll free +1-800-352-3535. Fax +1 813-621-7224. The hotel name says Brandon. The postal city says Tampa. Marriott calls Panfilo's Grille "our American restaurant in Brandon" and, in the next breath, describes a hotel bar "in Tampa, FL." AAA gives the same street: 10221 Princess Palm Ave, Tampa, FL, 33610. I think this is the whole confusion. People add "Tampa FL" onto "Sheraton Tampa Brandon" because the sign and the mailing city do not match, the same way they do with other Brandon businesses that sit in a Tampa ZIP.

AAA's location description is the only driving instruction I am willing to keep: Interstate 75, exit 260 southbound, exit 260B northbound, just west on SR 574 (Dr. Martin Luther King Jr Blvd), in Sabal Corporate Park. That is AAA, not something Marriott told me. I am not adding a minute count from the airport. AAA lists an airport shuttle as an amenity. Marriott, describing Seminole Hard Rock Casino, says the casino offers complimentary shuttle service and gives +1 813-514-2695, with a "5.0 Miles" figure attached to the casino, and also the phrase "just minutes from our hotel." Five miles and "just minutes" both come from Marriott. What Marriott never told me was that the hotel itself runs an airport shuttle. AAA says airport shuttle. Marriott talks about a casino shuttle. I thought people were struggling with those as if they were the same van. I would ask the hotel which shuttle is actually theirs before I planned on a pickup at Tampa International.

What Marriott itself states

Marriott advertises the hotel as "Fully Renovated." I am not going to date the renovation. They did not date it.

Pets: Marriott says pet-friendly, pets up to 75 lbs, with a small fee. It does not state the fee. AAA says pets accepted for an additional fee, and also does not give the amount. I am not filling one in.

Rooms, as Marriott describes them, not as a floor plan I measured: rooms with a balcony, leather headboards, wood accents, complimentary robe, pillowtop mattress, duvet, ergonomic chair, wooden desk, flat-screen with premium channels. A penthouse suite with a dining area, bar, and views. They say all room sizes are approximate. Sheraton Club lounge: Wi-Fi, seating, elevated food and beverage, complimentary for Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members plus one guest. When I asked, Marriott was also showing "No Accessible Rooms Available at this Hotel," with a note that there may be a limited number of accessible rooms and to select one while booking. I am reading that as inventory at that moment, not as a policy that the building has no accessible rooms. AAA's amenity list includes handicap accessible, and AAA says the hotel offers accessible amenities. Those two are not saying the same thing if you take Marriott's line as permanent. I would not.

AAA's inspector notes, which are AAA's, not Marriott's: interior corridors, 5 stories, smoke free, 266 units, business-park setting, traditional rooms, pool, fitness center, expansive meeting space. AAA's Diamond scores were room 3.5, bath 3.1, public areas 3.4. I am leaving those as AAA's scores.

Marriott never gave me check-in and check-out times. AAA's terms say check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM. I am attributing that to AAA only.

Pool: Marriott says yes, one outdoor pool, for hotel guests. AAA says outdoor pool and adds that it is heated. Marriott did not say heated. I am not merging them into "one heated pool" as if both used that adjective.

Fitness: Marriott says a complimentary fitness center for guests, with cardiovascular equipment, ellipticals, exercise bikes, free weights, strength equipment, treadmills, and weight machines. AAA lists a fitness center and an exercise room, and also tennis under sports. Marriott never mentioned tennis to me. Parking: AAA says on-site. Neither said whether that lot is free, gated, or charged. I am not inventing a parking price.

Dining, which is the part with actual hours

The on-property restaurant Marriott names is Panfilo's Grille, American, bar, casual dress. Hours as they give them:

Monday to Thursday: 6:30 AM–10:00 AM, 11:00 AM–1:00 PM, 4:00 PM–9:00 PM.
Friday and Saturday: 6:30 AM–10:00 AM, 11:00 AM–1:00 PM, 4:00 PM–10:00 PM.
Sunday: 6:30 AM–10:00 AM, 4:00 PM–9:00 PM.

Sunday does not have the 11 to 1 lunch block. I noticed that because it is easy to assume the weekday lunch block is every day. The restaurant's phone: +1 813-246-7121. Marriott says breakfast is available onsite at Panfilo's for a fee. No breakfast price. Room service: yes, Marriott says it is available. No room-service hours beyond what the restaurant and bar hours imply, and I am not going to infer them.

&More by Sheraton is the bar. Marriott calls it newly renovated, open seven days a week, everyday 4:00 PM–10:00 PM, kitchen closing at 9 p.m. on weekdays and 10 p.m. on weekends. So the room can still say 10:00 while the kitchen has already stopped on a weeknight. That is the kind of thing I would get wrong if I only remembered "open until 10."

Off-property, Marriott pointed to The Cheesecake Factory at 3.4 miles, open for lunch and dinner, casual, and Olive Garden at 3.7 miles. It also mentioned Umi Sushi & Seafood Buffet without a distance. I am not attaching a distance that was not given with the name.

Rates

I am not writing a rate as if I booked the room. Marriott described the rooms and did not give me a nightly number. AAA was quoting "hotel rates starting from $227" with taxes and fees calculated at checkout, plus a note that AAA/CAA members save and earn Marriott Bonvoy points on those rates. That $227 is a starting-from figure AAA gave on the day I asked. It is not a quote from Marriott's checkout, it is not a total, and I would not repeat it as the price of a specific night. If Marriott and AAA disagree tomorrow, I would believe the checkout I am actually paying, not this note.

What they call nearby, and what I would not trust as a walking plan

Marriott names Fairgrounds, Courtney Campbell Trail, Lettuce Lake Regional Park, Busch Gardens, and ZooTampa at Lowry Park as nearby. Their attraction rundown also includes addresses I recognize from elsewhere in Tampa: Florida State Fairgrounds area at 4800 US Hwy 301, Busch Gardens at 10165 McKinley Dr, ZooTampa at 1101 W Sligh Ave, The Florida Aquarium at 701 Channelside Dr, Curtis Hixon, Bayshore, Ybor, Henry B. Plant Museum. "Nearby" from a hotel is doing a lot of work. Courtney Campbell and Bayshore are across town from a Sabal Park address at exit 260. Marriott did not give me miles for those, except the messy figures I already separated out. Marriott also describes Topgolf Tampa, but the "4.7 Miles" figure I noted belonged to a workout place described just before it, so I am not pinning 4.7 miles on Topgolf.

iFLY at 10654 Palm River Rd, Tampa 33619, is among their attractions. That ZIP is the same 33619 as the Dave & Buster's on Estuary Lakes Drive, which is a different business and not a Sheraton amenity. I am only noting the ZIP overlap because people lump both under Tampa Brandon.

Marriott also says the hotel is near Busch Gardens, near MidFlorida Credit Union Amphitheatre, near the Tampa Riverwalk, and near Hard Rock Casino Tampa. Near is their word. The only mileage I am confident they attached is the 5.0 miles for the casino, and the restaurant miles.

Meetings: I never got the event spaces with room names and capacities, so I am not inventing ballroom sizes. AAA called the meeting space expansive. That is as far as I will go.
