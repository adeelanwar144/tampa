I went after the venue, not a theater district and not a casino. The answer comes from The Orpheum itself. It calls itself live music and events in Tampa Bay, and it gives its address as 14802 North Nebraska Avenue, Tampa, Florida 33613. The club repeats that address everywhere, with the phone (813) 248-9500 and the email cluborpheum813@gmail.com. The club says the room was originally in the historic Ybor City entertainment district and is now at home on Nebraska Avenue in North Tampa, minutes from the University of South Florida, and that it has been putting on live music for more than two decades. I am treating that Nebraska Avenue address as the current one because the club is the one saying it. I am not treating an old map pin on Seventh Avenue as current just because people still say "Ybor" next to the name.

What it is, and what it stopped being

The club is plain about the mix. It says more than twenty years and thousands of shows, national and local bands, and also professional wrestling, art shows, club events, charity fundraisers, and dance competitions. It gives a stage of 16 feet deep, 26 feet wide, and 11 feet of trim height, flown mains, a pile of subs, and a Midas M-32 at front of house with monitors run from the same desk. I noted that because people talk about this place like it is a historic Orpheum palace with a balcony and a chandelier. The spec sheet is a club PA. It is not a restored vaudeville house.

The move is the part I kept running into, so I pinned down the dates. The last concert at the Ybor room was a sold-out Suicide Silence show on May 25, 2022, and the last time the doors were open at 1915 E 7th Ave was Emo Night Brooklyn on Friday, May 27. The new address was the same one the club uses now, 14802 N Nebraska Ave, a move of about 10 miles north. Owner Jerry "Lazy" Dufrain said at the time he expected a soft launch on June 11 for Destroy Boys, and he described the drive from Ybor as 10 or 11 minutes because he had been doing it constantly. The new room is a five-minute walk from Skipper's Smokehouse, and Dufrain would not put a capacity number on the record. I am not going to invent one. The building had been Southern Ground Brick Bar & Party Deck, he meant to keep the back wooden patio, and the new room was to be indoor-outdoor rather than a copy of the old Ybor box. It was not the club's first move. In 2011 it left a small corner at 8th Street and Avenida Republica de Cuba for Seventh Avenue.

The club itself does not dwell on that Ybor ending. It just says formerly Ybor, now North Tampa. The dates and the old street are history. The address the club gives is the one you drive to. If those two ever diverge again, I would trust the address the club gives and then call the number, because this place has already changed buildings twice.

The word Orpheum sounds like one famous theater. The schedule can point at more than one room.

The other Orpheums, and the other Tampa rooms people mean

Orpheum is an old circuit name, and historic Orpheum theaters in other cities get mixed in with this club. I did not look into those houses and I am not going to paste their streets into a Tampa note. If the address in front of you is not 14802 North Nebraska Avenue, Tampa, Florida 33613, it is the wrong Orpheum. Tampa also has Tampa Theatre, a different downtown movie palace, and people mash the names because both sound like "the old theater in Tampa." This note is not that building. The Orpheum never claims to be it.

The related words people attach to the name are the other mess. Rock, show, and tickets belong here, in the sense that this is a rock-and-everything-else club and the schedule is how you find a show. Hard is the trap. People say hard next to Tampa and land on the Seminole Hard Rock, which is a hotel and a casino, not this room. Tampa nightlife usually gets described as Seminole Hard Rock lumped in with Channelside and Ybor, so music and casino questions end up in the same conversation. I am not sending anyone to the casino for a club show, and I am not pretending the Orpheum is inside a hotel.

Rays, Bucs, stadium, Lightning, and an AMC are the same kind of collision. They are other buildings with other tickets. The Buccaneers are at Raymond James Stadium and the Lightning at Benchmark International Arena. The Rays I have heard placed both across the bay in St. Petersburg and at George M. Steinbrenner Field. I am not resolving the Rays. I am saying that if a ticket is for hockey, football, or baseball, it is not The Orpheum, even if the conversation started with the club's name. Visit Tampa Bay puts the Lightning at Benchmark International Arena, 401 Channelside Drive. Visit Florida still calls it Amalie Arena for concerts. Those names are the arena fight, not this club. An AMC is a movie theater. I did not need a movie address to know it is not 14802 Nebraska.

What I thought people were struggling with

The old address. 1915 E 7th Ave is a real former door, and it closed at the end of May 2022. If a map pin, an old flyer, or a ride-share favorite still says Ybor, that is the room they left. The club says North Tampa now.

The palace expectation. The stage is 26 feet wide. Wrestling and dance competitions sit next to the bands in how the club describes itself. If someone wanted a seated historic theater, they had the wrong proper noun.

The schedule that mixes rooms. I watched it do that on October 4. District 8 was on the same schedule as The Orpheum.

The distance. The club says minutes from USF and does not give a mileage. I was given 10 miles north and a 10 or 11 minute drive from Ybor, plus a five-minute walk from Skipper's. I am leaving all three phrasings in the note because they are not the same kind of claim, and none of them is a promise about traffic on a show night.

Tickets. I would buy for the specific event directly through the club, then check that the address is Nebraska Avenue. I would not buy a Lightning ticket, a Bucs ticket, a Rays ticket, or a Hard Rock hotel package and expect it to scan at this door.
