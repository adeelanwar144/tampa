Side Splitters, and which door people mean

People say side splitters tampa, and the flipped version, tampa side splitters. The club itself runs more than one room, and they are not all the same room. I am writing this on Sunday, October 4, 2026. I am not inventing a show time or a ticket price. I was not given either one for tonight.

The Tampa room

The club gives the same address everywhere: 12938 North Dale Mabry Highway, Tampa, FL 33618. The phone is 813-960-1197. The email is livecomedy@aol.com. That is the club. Side Splitters says it is celebrating 33 years in Tampa Bay, and that it won Best Comedy Club, Best Comedy Open Mic, and Best Comedy Venue in the Best of the Bay awards in 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. It calls itself one of the nation's premier comedy clubs. I am repeating the claim as the club states it, not as a ranking I verified.

The way they tell you to get a seat is the same for every location. Pick the comedian, pick the date and the showtime, then pick the seats. The showtime is a step in that checkout. It is not a clock time I was ever given up front. The club says you do not have to buy ahead for every show, but many sell out, and they recommend buying ahead. Most shows go about 90 minutes. Arrive about 30 minutes before showtime. Parking, they say, is free, and there is a lot in front of the club. That is the only parking fact I am keeping. I did not measure the lot.

The two-item minimum is the rule I would have missed. The club says each ticket holder has to buy two items inside the showroom, and those items can be food or drinks. Nobody says what an item costs. The menu does not either. They say they have a full bar and "great munchies," and that service runs through the show, and then they repeat the Dale Mabry address. No prices, no drink list, no food list. I am not going to guess a beer. If the minimum is the thing that surprises people, it is because the club names the rule and gives you nothing you can budget.

What was on for this weekend, without times

For the Tampa room, the upcoming shows came as three date ranges and no clock. Joe List, marked special event, Saturday, October 3, 2026. Ahren Belisle, marked special event, Sunday, October 4 through Monday, October 5, 2026. Tim Dillon, marked special event, Wednesday, October 7 through Monday, October 12, 2026. Today is the 4th, so the name on the Tampa stage for this date is Ahren Belisle, as a two-day span. I do not know if that means one show tonight, two shows, or a show tomorrow only. Nobody said. I would confirm the showtime for that specific event before I drove up Dale Mabry.

The club also keeps comedian bios, which are not a calendar. I saw writeups for Ms. Pat, K-von, Paul Virzi, Daryl Wright, Angelo Tsarouchas, Josh Wolf, Tim Dillon, Shane Gillis, and Dusty Slay, plus Comic's Night Out. K-von's blurb says they sold out last time and to get tickets early. None of those bios gave me an October 4 clock or a dollar. I am not turning a bio into a booking.

Open mic, and two emails that do not match

Comic's Night Out is the open mic. The club's FAQ says each performer gets 5 minutes, and the signup email there is comicsnightout@gmail.com. The club's own description of the same night disagrees. It claims a local paper's best comedy open mic award in 2016, 2017, 2018, and 2020, it says all tickets include your first drink, and the signup email there is operations@sidesplitterscomedy.com. The Best of the Bay years, 2021 through 2025, are a third set of years and a different award name. I am not merging them into one trophy. If I wanted to perform, I would send both emails or call 813-960-1197, because the club itself does not agree on the inbox. The "first drink included" line is only in that description, not in the FAQ, so I would not promise a new comic a free drink off the FAQ.

The other rooms the same brand runs

The brand has three locations: Tampa, The Grove at Wesley Chapel, and St. Pete. Wesley Chapel is a real second room, and it is not the Tampa address. The club says the Tampa Bay club has expanded to Wesley Chapel, and it calls that room Side Splitters Comedy Theatre. The address there is 6333 Wesley Grove Blvd, Theater #7, Wesley Chapel, FL 33544. The phone is 813-723-2170, not the Dale Mabry number. The email is the same livecomedy@aol.com. Upcoming there, again with dates and no times: "Big Irish" Jay Hollingsworth on Saturday, October 3; Dish Rack Comedy on Sunday, October 4; Misha Brown on Tuesday, October 6. So on the Sunday I am writing this, the Tampa room has Ahren Belisle across October 4 and 5, and the Wesley Chapel room has Dish Rack Comedy on October 4. Same brand, same night, different show, different city. That is where people go wrong. Side splitters tampa is Dale Mabry. The Grove is Wesley Chapel even though the area code is still 813.

St. Pete is the location I do not trust. The club says they are expanding and hosting shows at Coastal Creative in downtown St. Pete. The address given is 2201 1st Ave S, St. Petersburg, FL 33712. The phone is the Tampa phone, 813-960-1197, not a St. Pete number of its own. The only upcoming event they show there is Mike E. Winfield at Coastal Creative, Friday, September 2, 2022. That is four years old. I am not going to tell anyone there is a Side Splitters show in St. Petersburg this weekend. The club still names St. Pete as a location. The schedule for it is not current. If someone sends you to 2201 1st Avenue South, check the date on the event before you cross the bridge.

What the club will not tell you

I went looking for hours, prices, and an age rule, because those are the things that wreck a comedy night. The club does not post a weekly hours grid. It does not post a cover charge. It does not say whether a reserved seat is required or whether you can walk in. Their contact information is the address, the phone, and the email, twice. Their main greeting is basically a question, where do you want to go for a laugh, which is their way of admitting there is more than one room. They sell gift cards. I was not given a gift-card amount, so I do not have one. They also have a way to book a comic and a comics corner. I did not treat those as a public show list.

The practical version, for me, is short. The Tampa club is 12938 North Dale Mabry Highway, 33618, 813-960-1197. The lot in front is the parking they say is free. Plan on two items per person inside, about 90 minutes, and about 30 minutes early, and buy ahead if the name matters, because they say many shows sell out. Do not use the Wesley Chapel dates as the Tampa dates. Do not count on the St. Pete location at all until it shows an event from this decade. And do not believe a ticket price you did not see in the seat map. I did not see one.

People asking about side splitters tampa also bring up Hard Rock, a Seminole casino, Rays, a stadium, Bucs, Lightning, AMC, a hotel, and a show that is not this show. Dale Mabry is a long road. The comedy club is the 33618 address, not a casino and not a ballpark. If I were going tonight I would call 813-960-1197, confirm that Ahren Belisle is actually on the Tampa stage and not only on a date range, ask what time the door is, and ask what the two items are going to cost, because the club would not say.
