# Tampa Flea Market: Big Top Closed, Where to Go Instead

I went looking for a flea market in Tampa under every name people use: tampa flea market tampa fl, tampa flea market, flea market tampa, florida flea market tampa, flea markets in tampa. I expected one obvious place with that name on the gate. I did not find a market, open now, whose real name is Tampa Flea Market—and I am not inventing one.

What I found hunting the name from Tampa: a famous market that closed, a large weekend market that is real but not inside the city, and a Saturday market that is inside Tampa ZIP 33605 but is not a classic junk flea. **Last updated:** November 2, 2026.

## Quick Answers

- **Big Top Flea Market:** permanently closed April 26, 2020—do not drive to Fowler for booths
- **Oldsmar Flea Market:** the acres-of-booths weekend market—180 Racetrack Rd, Oldsmar, FL 34677; Sat–Sun 8 a.m.–3 p.m.; free parking
- **Ybor City Saturday Market:** inside Tampa 33605—produce and makers, not a classic flea; seasonal end times
- **No live “Tampa Flea Market” brand** matching the search phrase as a gate name

## Big Top Is What People Still Mean—and It Is Gone

The name that kept coming up as “Tampa’s flea market” is Big Top. In January 2020, management told vendors the last day open would be April 26, 2020, at 5 p.m., after 26 years. Vendors could return April 27–May 3 to clear stalls. After May 3, Big Top was permanently closed.

Local photo history: Big Top started in the early 1960s on Dale Mabry Highway in Tampa, later moved to a larger Fowler Avenue site in Thonotosassa, and was called one of the largest flea markets in the Southeast. The “26 years” line matches the Fowler era more than the entire Dale Mabry history. After the 2020 sale and rezoning, the site became the Livano Uptown apartment community. As of 2026, remaining commercial parcels on the old footprint are redeveloping—including a Wawa planned for a Fowler Avenue parcel with construction underway toward a mid-2027 opening. There is no live Big Top operation with current hours. If a directory still shows a phone and a Fowler address, treat it as a leftover. The mini-donut memories are history. The market is not there.

## Oldsmar: The Big Weekend Flea That Is Actually Open

The market that matches what people mean when they say flea market tampa and picture acres of booths is Oldsmar Flea Market. It is not in Tampa. Address: 180 Racetrack Road, Oldsmar, Florida 34677. Office phone 813-855-2587.

**Hours:** Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Monday–Friday the office lists closed. A limited number of vendors may be open on weekdays—Wacky Wizard Hardware, Everything Birds, and T&D Golf—check those vendors directly; that is not “the whole market is open Wednesday.”

**Parking:** free for visitors.

**Dogs:** welcome only if carried or in a stroller.

**ATMs:** one in building D, one in building H. Several vendors take cards—which implies not all do. Bring cash backup.

Goods people find without needing a treasure map: snacks, fresh produce, fashion, vintage clothing, golf equipment, sunglasses, rugs, furniture, classic video games. Featured booths when I looked included a family cigar seller in the J West breezeway, a hemp and glass shop, and a luggage booth in building I East.

**Fact update from older “28 acres” talk:** in July 2026 the market announced it would keep operating after selling the southern portion of the property for apartments, continuing from the remaining **16-acre** portion, still every weekend 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Confirm on [Oldsmar Flea Market](https://www.oldsmarfleamarket.com/).

That is the struggle with “florida flea market tampa.” People want this scale, and the open answer is a weekend drive to Oldsmar with a hard stop at 3 p.m. I would go Saturday morning, not Tuesday, and I would not bring a dog that has to walk.

From downtown, build time for the drive—this is not a Riverwalk hop. Pair with a beach day using [Tampa to Clearwater](/guides/tampa-to-clearwater) only if you want a long Saturday; otherwise keep Oldsmar as its own errand.

## Ybor City Saturday Market: In Tampa, Not a Classic Flea

If tampa flea market tampa fl means “inside the city, in the ZIP,” the recurring market I could confirm is the Ybor City Saturday Market. It is not a flea in the Big Top sense. It has operated since 2000 and calls itself the largest continually operating outdoor market in the Tampa Bay area—local produce, gourmet foods, bread, herbs, plants, pet treats, honey, cigars, gifts, art, jewelry, woodwork, and candles.

**Location:** 1901 North 19th Street, Tampa, FL 33605 (state agriculture department and Ybor chamber agree). Phone 813-241-2442.

**Hours:** every Saturday, year-round, rain or shine—October through April 9 a.m.–3 p.m.; May through September 9 a.m.–1 p.m. They post updates for lightning or tropical storms. No Sunday version.

A cool-season Saturday runs until 3. A summer Saturday is a four-hour market done at 1 p.m. If someone calls the Ybor market “the flea market,” they are mixing a producer and artisan market with a booth flea. Both can be worth a morning. They are not the same errand. For more on the district, see [Ybor City](/guides/ybor-city-tampa-fl-33605).

## Three Mix-Ups I Kept Seeing

**Dead map pin.** Searching tampa flea market still drags up Big Top. Last public day was April 26, 2020. Showing up on Fowler for antiques is how you find apartments (and future Wawa construction), not booths.

**City line.** Oldsmar is the acres-of-booths answer, open Saturday and Sunday 8–3, free parking, dogs carried, weekdays mostly dark. Calling it a Tampa flea market is a habit, not an address.

**Expecting one market to be both.** Ybor’s Saturday market is in 33605 on 19th Street and shuts earlier in hot months. It will not scratch the itch for a football field of used tools. Oldsmar will not scratch the itch for a walk from Centro Ybor.

## Tips From My Search

Go to Oldsmar when you want the flea—weekend, early, cash as backup because they bothered to tell you where the ATMs are. Go to the Ybor Saturday market when you want produce, bread, and small-maker stalls and you are already in the historic district; leave by 1 p.m. May–September. Do not go back to Big Top. There is nothing there to go back to.

For indoor shopping instead, [International Plaza](/shopping/international-plaza-tampa) is the Westshore mall day. For a food-hall morning in Tampa Heights, [Armature Works](/food-and-drink/armature-works-tampa) is a different kind of browse.

## FAQ

### Is there a Tampa Flea Market open right now?

There is no major market currently operating under the exact name “Tampa Flea Market.” Big Top closed in 2020. Use Oldsmar for a classic flea or Ybor Saturday Market for a city producer market.

### What happened to Big Top Flea Market?

It closed permanently on April 26, 2020. The Fowler / Thonotosassa site was redeveloped; apartments and new commercial projects occupy the footprint.

### Where is the closest big flea market to Tampa?

Oldsmar Flea Market at 180 Racetrack Road, Oldsmar—Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m.–3 p.m., free parking.

### Is the Ybor market a flea market?

It is an outdoor Saturday market focused on produce, food, and artisans—not a classic multi-acre junk-and-tools flea like Oldsmar or historic Big Top.

### Can I bring my dog to Oldsmar?

Only if the dog is carried or in a stroller.

## Final Thoughts

The search phrase wants one gate. Tampa gives you a closed legend, a weekend drive to Oldsmar, and a Saturday produce market in Ybor. Once I stopped hunting for a living “Tampa Flea Market” sign, the plan got simple: Oldsmar for the flea, Ybor for the makers, Fowler only if you like looking at apartments.
