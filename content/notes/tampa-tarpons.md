# Tampa Tarpons: Yankees Single-A Baseball at Steinbrenner

Every Tampa sports search dumps Rays tickets, Bucs kickoffs, Lightning games, and Hard Rock shows into the same pile. The **Tampa Tarpons** are none of those. They are a Minor League Baseball club—the Single-A affiliate of the New York Yankees in the Florida State League—with their own tickets, schedule, and home at George M. Steinbrenner Field.

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026.

## What the Name Means

The current club began in 1994 as the Tampa Yankees. On December 11, 2017, MiLB announced the rebrand to Tampa Tarpons for 2018, bringing the Tarpons name back to the Florida State League after a long gap. An earlier Tampa Tarpons franchise played 1957–1987 and won titles in 1957 and 1961. Live tickets today mean the Yankees affiliate, not a museum of the 1960s club.

MLB’s Steinbrenner Field profile lists championships in 1994, 2001, 2004, 2009, and 2010 under the affiliate history and notes the 2021 move from Class A Advanced to Single-A with the rest of the Florida State League. Colors at rebrand: Legends Navy, Tarpon Silver, Gulf Blue. The fish is intentional—Gulf Coast game fish known for fighting when hooked; logos nod at a 1970s Tarpons look with a bat against pinstripe cues. Kids-club mascot name: King Ripple.

Notable alumni pipelines MLB names include Aaron Judge, Derek Jeter, Mariano Rivera, Luis Severino, David Robertson, Mike Lowell, Tim Raines, and Luis Sojo—Yankees lineage, not Rays.

## The 2025 “Tank” Season (History, Not a Forever Rule)

In 2025 the Tarpons did not play most home dates in the main bowl. On February 4, 2025, the club announced it would play on **Community Field** at the GMS complex—the side field by the tennis courts—after offering the main stadium to the Tampa Bay Rays for that hurricane-displaced season. Side-field capacity was given as about 1,000, with lights added for nights. An April 4, 2025 follow-up nicknamed the temporary home **“The Tank,”** said BayCare presented the season, and priced many Tank tickets at $2 Tuesdays and $5 Wednesday–Sunday when bought ahead. The Tarpons still took the main stadium for three dates (May 17, July 4, August 30), including Independence Day and Fan Appreciation Night, with a 3-Game Stadium Plan on offer.

That arrangement is **2025 history**. It explains why people mixed Rays and Tarpons pins that year. It is not a promise about every future home date. By fall 2026 the venue was still marketing Steinbrenner Field as home of the Tarpons, pushing 2026 printable schedules and already advertising 2027. On September 9, 2026, the venue noted the Tarpons were back in the playoffs chasing a first FSL title since 2010. Nobody handed me a diamond-by-diamond map for every 2026 date—check the specific ticket for field and gate.

For the Rays’ 2026 Tropicana Field home, see [Tampa Bay Rays](/events/tampa-bay-rays) and [Tropicana Field](/events/tropicana-field).

## What a Night at Steinbrenner Feels Like vs MLB

This is Minor League Baseball: affiliate prospects, Florida State League pacing, and a complex built for Yankees spring crowds. Monument Park and the oversized gift shop lean into Yankees identity on purpose. You are not walking into Tropicana Field’s clear-bag dome culture or Raymond James’ no-bag NFL screening—confirm Steinbrenner’s event rules for your date rather than borrowing another venue’s inches. Family plans, birthday packages, and kids-club nights are part of how the Tarpons sell the season; they are also why weekday Tank-era pricing in 2025 felt like a different product than a weekend MLB night across the bay.

If you are visiting Tampa only once and can see one baseball game, decide whether you want MLB branding in St. Petersburg or a Yankees-affiliate night in Tampa. They scratch different itches. Mixing them because both said “Tampa Bay” on a tourism brochure is how people buy the wrong clear bag and the wrong bridge.

## FAQ

### Are the Tampa Tarpons the Rays?

No. The Tarpons are the Yankees’ Single-A Florida State League affiliate. The Rays are the MLB club that plays at Tropicana Field (and spent 2025 at Steinbrenner as a temporary home). The shared 2025 address is the whole reason the nicknames collided—not proof they are one franchise.

### Where do the Tarpons play?

George M. Steinbrenner Field, 1 Steinbrenner Drive, Tampa, FL 33614. Confirm the specific diamond on your ticket if alternate fields are listed.

### Why did I hear they played on a side field?

In 2025 most Tarpons home games used Community Field (“The Tank”) while the Rays used the main stadium. That was a one-season arrangement tied to the Tropicana Field roof crisis.

### Is Steinbrenner Field only for the Tarpons?

No. It is also the Yankees’ spring-training home and has been used for other baseball tenants and events. Match your ticket to the date.

### How do I buy tickets?

Through Tarpons / GMS Field official account tools for mobile tickets. Prefer the club’s links over random resale screenshots.
