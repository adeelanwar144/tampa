## Amalie Is the Old Name

On August 13, 2025, Vinik Sports Group and Benchmark International announced a multi-year naming-rights deal. **AMALIE Arena became Benchmark International Arena** that day. The building debuted in 1996 as a publicly owned downtown venue with Vinik Sports Group holding the lease. AMALIE Motor Oil stayed on as a corporate partner without keeping the name on the door.

The rebrand covers exterior and interior signage. The premium level is the Benchmark International Club Level; **The Mark** is described as its signature all-inclusive experience (no public price in the materials I used). The deal also references more than $3 million in nonprofit contributions—that is partnership money, not a ticket price.

You will still hear “Amalie” from locals and from older downtown business directories. Trust the Channelside address and the Benchmark signs. For ticket-buying detail and fraud warnings, see [Tampa Lightning tickets](/events/tampa-lightning-tickets).

**Breakdown the team published:**

- Western Conference: 32 games (each opponent twice)
- Split: 42 home, 42 road

**Early-season anchors from the announcement:**

- Season open: Oct 1, 2026 at New York Rangers (Madison Square Garden)—club later recorded a 5–1 road loss

**Homestands and travel:**

- Two six-game homestands: Nov 14–27 and Feb 12–23
- Longest road trip: four games (four times)
- November: eight home games (most of any month)
- December and February: seven home games each

## Downtown Context

Channelside puts you near Water Street dining and the cruise / aquarium end of downtown—not next to Raymond James on Dale Mabry and not next to Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg. A Lightning night and a Bucs noon kickoff are different parking problems in different parts of the metro. Pairing hockey with a Riverwalk walk is realistic; pairing it with a same-evening Tropicana Field first pitch usually is not.

Flying in: [Tampa International Airport](/getting-around/tampa-international-airport) sits west of downtown—budget the Crosstown / downtown approach separately. For a map-level orientation of city vs county pins, see [Tampa map](/getting-around/tampa-map).

Water Street and Channelside restaurants fill up on theme nights and weekend puck drops. Make a dinner reservation under the arena’s current name so the host stand does not hunt “Amalie” on an old note. If your group is split between a concert and a hockey night in the same week, remember they share an address but not always a box-office clock or a cashless merchandise rule.

## Getting to Channelside Without Mixing Venues

From western Tampa or the airport, downtown approaches via the Crosstown (Selmon) or I-275 depend on your start point—live navigation beats a memorized ramp letter when events stack. From South Tampa, Bayshore-to-downtown routes dump you toward the waterfront cluster where the arena sits with other Channelside attractions. From Dale Mabry after a stadium event, you are crossing a large slice of the city; do not assume “both are Tampa sports” means a short hop.

Rideshare pins should say Benchmark International Arena or 401 Channelside Drive. If a driver only knows “Amalie,” add the street. Parking products change with concerts versus hockey—confirm the event listing rather than a friend’s memory of a 2024 show.

## FAQ

### Where do the Tampa Bay Lightning play?

Benchmark International Arena, 401 Channelside Drive, Tampa, FL 33602. The building was formerly named AMALIE Arena until August 13, 2025.

### How many games are in the 2026–27 season?

84 regular-season games—42 home and 42 road—per the team’s July 16, 2026 announcement.

### When was the 2026–27 home opener?

Saturday, October 3, 2026, against the Washington Capitals, after an October 1 road game at the Rangers.

### Are Lightning tickets mobile?

Yes. Games, concerts, and most arena events are mobile entry through Ticketmaster.

### Is the arena the same as Raymond James Stadium?

No. Raymond James is the Buccaneers / USF football stadium on Dale Mabry. Lightning hockey is downtown on Channelside.
