# Terra Gaucha Tampa: My Honest Guide (Dale Mabry Churrascaria)

Terra Gaucha Tampa is the South Tampa Brazilian steakhouse at **1108 South Dale Mabry Highway, Tampa, FL 33629**—fire-roasted cuts carved tableside, a Gourmet Hot and Cold Table, desserts, and handcrafted cocktails under what the restaurant calls the Ultimate Churrasco Experience. People go for a full rodizio night, a hot-and-cold-table-only meal, a weekend lunch that prices closer to dinner, Tuesday live music, or a private/semi-private group event.

Phone **(813) 999-4332**. Email office@terragaucha.net. Confirm city and pricing on [Terra Gaucha Tampa](https://terragaucha.com/location-tampa/). **Last updated:** November 2, 2026.

I started with the longer search phrase because the short name mixes this room up with every other Brazilian steakhouse nearby—especially [Texas de Brazil](/food-and-drink/texas-de-brazil-tampa) on Boy Scout in Westshore and Terra Gaucha Jacksonville on Southside Boulevard. I would put the street number in the rideshare note so the pin lands on Dale Mabry.

## Before You Book

- **Confirm the city** on the reservation (Jacksonville, Stamford, and other cities exist).  
- **Pick a price tier**—full churrasco, table only, or chicken/pork/seafood.  
- **Weekday lunch ≠ weekend lunch.**  
- **Parties over 15** use group dining.  

## Menu Highlights

From the Tampa location page and FAQs:

- **Full Churrasco Experience** — premium fire-roasted cuts carved tableside plus the Gourmet Hot and Cold Table  
- **Gourmet Hot and Cold Table only** — a real purchase tier, not a free graze  
- **Chicken, pork, and seafood** option — includes the hot and cold table  
- Desserts named on materials include Brazilian flan and papaya cream  
- Large portion of the menu described as gluten-free; tell the server about allergies  
- Vegetarian path: hot and cold table with fresh dishes and vegetables ([FAQs](https://terragaucha.com/faqs/))  
- No separate kids menu; ages 0–5 complimentary, 6–12 half price  

Live music in Tampa: **Tuesdays 7:00–9:00 p.m.** (schedule may change without notice).

## Atmosphere

The Tampa page describes a refined nightlife feel in South Tampa near US 92, with private and semi-private spaces for events. Group dining materials emphasize a refined atmosphere, premium cuts, Brazilian-inspired dishes, and a dedicated server for larger parties. This is a sit-down churrascaria experience, not a food-hall counter and not a quick Westshore mall dinner. If your group wants a quieter corner, ask about semi-private space when you book through group dining—those rooms are part of how the Tampa location markets itself for celebrations and business nights.

Terra Gaucha also operates in Jacksonville, Stamford, Indianapolis, Omaha, Rockville, and Phoenix. Tampa is its own restaurant on Dale Mabry. If a confirmation says Southside Boulevard or a Connecticut mall, you booked the wrong city.

## Brunch / Lunch

There is no separate “brunch” label on the Tampa page—**lunch** is the midday service:

- **Mon–Thu:** 11:30 a.m.–2:00 p.m.  
- **Fri:** 11:30 a.m.–2:30 p.m.  
- **Sat–Sun:** 11:30 a.m.–3:00 p.m.  

Weekend lunch prices sit much closer to dinner than weekday lunch (see Price Range).

## Happy Hour

**Terra Bites** (happy hour) is **Monday–Friday 4:30–6:30 p.m.** at patio or bar—and FAQs state it is **exclusive to Terra Gaucha Stamford**. There is no Tampa Terra Bites window. Expect a weekday gap between lunch end and dinner start (dinner begins 5:00 p.m. Mon–Thu).

## Reservations

Reservations are **not required**, but the restaurant encourages booking—especially weekends and holidays—at [terragaucha.com/reservations](https://terragaucha.com/reservations) (FAQs). Parties **over 15** go through [group dining](https://terragaucha.com/groupdining) for private/semi-private rooms, buyouts, and packages (birthdays, engagements, rehearsal dinners, end-of-year events, business meetings).

## Where People Get Stuck

The first stuck point is the name and city—booking without reading the city on the confirmation is how Jacksonville and Stamford reservations happen. The second is the price tier: full churrasco, table only, and chicken-pork-seafood are three different checks, and weekend lunch is not weekday lunch. The third is the afternoon gap with no Tampa Terra Bites. The fourth is a party of 16 trying a regular reservation instead of group dining. The fifth is building a birthday around Tuesday music that might not run that week.

If booking gives you trouble, call (813) 999-4332 or email office@terragaucha.net. For a same-night party-size change, call rather than assuming the inbox is watched during dinner service.

South Tampa is the neighborhood context the restaurant gives; US 92 is the road context. Westshore / Boy Scout is a different Brazilian steakhouse night entirely—use [Texas de Brazil Tampa](/food-and-drink/texas-de-brazil-tampa) if that is the pin you meant.

## FAQ

### Is Terra Gaucha Tampa the same as Texas de Brazil?

No. Terra Gaucha is on South Dale Mabry in South Tampa. Texas de Brazil is on Boy Scout Boulevard in Westshore.

### How much does it cost?

Dinner full churrasco posts at $65.95; weekday lunch full experience $42.95; weekend lunch full experience $59.95—confirm on the Tampa location page.

### Do I need a reservation?

Not required; book for weekends and holidays. Over 15 guests: group dining.

### Is there happy hour in Tampa?

Terra Bites is Stamford-only per FAQs. No Tampa happy hour window.

### What should I wear?

Smart casual recommended; no formal dress code.

## Final Thoughts

Terra Gaucha Tampa is the Dale Mabry churrascaria, not the Westshore Texas de Brazil and not Jacksonville. Get the pin right, pick the price tier on purpose, and do not treat weekend lunch like the weekday deal. Tuesday music is a bonus when it runs—not a promise to build a whole night around. Recheck the Tampa location page the day you go; pricing is posted as subject to change without notice.
