# Tampa Buccaneers at Raymond James: Tickets, Parking, Bags

People say Tampa Bucs, Bucs Tampa, and “James Stadium” when they drop the first name. They all mean the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Raymond James Stadium—4201 N. Dale Mabry Hwy, Tampa, FL 33607—not a baseball park, not a casino night, and not the Lightning arena downtown.

This is the practical gameday guide: tickets, parking, the no-bag rule, rideshare at Steinbrenner Field, and how to read what is still left on the 2026 schedule. **Last updated:** November 2, 2026 (policies and remaining 2026 dates checked against team and stadium pages).

## What “Tampa Buccaneers” Points To

The franchise became an NFL club in 1976, plays in the NFC South, and lists Super Bowl XXXVII (2002 season) among its championships. The stadium debuted September 20, 1998, and takes its name from Raymond James Financial—a St. Petersburg–based firm—not a person. Naming rights have run since 1998 and continue through the 2027 season, per USF athletics’ stadium overview. Capacity figures online jump around; the number I am willing to repeat from USF is about 65,000 seats. The north end zone pirate ship is a Buccaneers-owned replica closed to the public during events, with cannons on Bucs game days.

USF Bulls football also calls Raymond James home. There is not a second “USF stadium” next door. If your ticket says Bulls, ReliaQuest Bowl, Gasparilla Bowl, or Buccaneers, you are still on Dale Mabry. If your ticket says Rays, you want Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg—see [Tropicana Field](/events/tropicana-field) and [Tampa Bay Rays](/events/tampa-bay-rays). If it says Lightning, you want Benchmark International Arena on Channelside—see [Tampa Bay Lightning](/events/tampa-bay-lightning).

## Tickets and the South Plaza Window

All stadium events are mobile tickets. Load them into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet before you reach the gate. Screenshots fail. Every guest needs their own ticket once you are inside; turn brightness up. Buccaneers ticket questions go to the club (813-879-2827). USF ticket operations are separate (813-974-3002 / 1-800-GO-BULLS). Other stadium events run through Ticketmaster.

The ticket office sits in the South Plaza. Stadium materials describe it between the Suzuki Marine and Fifth Third Bank gates next to the stadium office, and at least one Steelers-game listing said between Gates C and D. Lost-ticket language sends you to the office between Suzuki Marine and “D.” I am not declaring those doors identical. On event day, follow the plaza signs—not a memory of last year’s gate letter. The office is open on event days only. Normal stadium hours: 813-350-6500; event day: 813-350-6501.

Kids policies change by event type. Buccaneers and Gasparilla Bowl: every age needs a ticket. USF and ReliaQuest Bowl: three and under free if they do not take a seat. Monster Jam / Supercross / many concerts: two and under free under the same condition. Read the specific event page.

## Bag Policy: No Bag, Not “Clear Bag”

Raymond James runs a **no-bag policy for every event**. You may bring **one clutch no larger than 4.5″ by 6.5″**. That clutch does **not** have to be clear. Larger bags—even clear ones—stay out. Fanny packs are listed among prohibited items. Lockers for a small fee sit on Tom McEwen Boulevard, south of the Suzuki Marine gate. Everything else goes back to the car. Fans who refuse wanding are not admitted. Re-entry is only for a medical emergency through a Tampa Sports Authority supervisor. Strollers and umbrellas stay out. The stadium is open-air, rain or shine. Shirt required; heels and sandals are discouraged.

This is the opposite of Tropicana Field’s clear-bag rule. Do not pack a Rays bag for a Bucs kickoff.

## Parking, Tailgating Limits, and Steinbrenner Rideshare

All lots are cashless and contactless (major credit cards). There is no single posted price—every event sets its own rate and lot-open time. The A-to-Z guide cites roughly 10,000 on-site and 15,000 off-site spaces. Advance parking is a separate purchase when offered. ADA parking is first come, first served on arrival with valid plates/ID—you cannot buy those spots ahead.

**RVs / buses / campers:** Buccaneers games use Publix Lot 14 off MLK. Most other Raymond James events use Lot 8 off Himes. Nothing overnight. Passes for RVs are usually sold in advance.

**Tailgating reality check:** Stadium parking rules prohibit recreational games in the lots (throwing footballs and the like). Plan food and chairs around the posted lot rules for your event; do not treat the asphalt like a park.

**Rideshare is the classic trap.** For Buccaneers games, Uber and Lyft drop-off and pickup are at **George M. Steinbrenner Field**, not a random Dale Mabry curb. Taxi, bus, and limo staging use that mass-transit / non-disabled drop area too. Concerts and heavy nights can move pickup—check the event listing. Steinbrenner Field is also Yankees spring training and Tampa Tarpons territory (see [Tampa Tarpons](/events/tampa-tarpons)); on Bucs night it is a rideshare pin, not a football stadium. Roadside help in the lots: 813-350-6501.

Bicycle racks sit by docks A–D; the stadium is not responsible for missing bikes. A “decide to ride” designated-driver booth can hand out soft-drink coupons and, the stadium says, sometimes a discounted rideshare home—useful, not a transit plan.

## Getting There Without Guessing the Wrong Building

Dale Mabry is the spine. From I-275, follow stadium / Dale Mabry signing for your direction of travel. Build buffer for lot traffic. If you are coming from downtown after a Riverwalk morning, you are leaving the Channelside / Water Street cluster for northwest Tampa—different trip than [Benchmark International Arena](/events/tampa-bay-lightning) or a hotel near Orient Road. Flying in first? Land at [Tampa International Airport](/getting-around/tampa-international-airport), then plan Dale Mabry timing separately from terminal traffic.

Team store hours in the A-to-Z guide are roughly Mon–Fri 10 a.m.–6 p.m., Sat noon–5 p.m., Sunday closed—except event mornings when the store opens with the lot clock (Packers day opened 9:30 a.m.). Concessions are cashless; reverse ATMs sit at select spots. Alcohol: 21 with ID, two per person per purchase, none in or out; sales on main/upper concourses generally stop around the start of the fourth quarter (club areas may run later—guides disagree slightly on third vs fourth). Guest services text: 813-680-2827. Wi-Fi network: bucswifi. Ticket resale on stadium property (lots included) is prohibited.

Tours run most Mondays–Thursdays and select Fridays at 2:00 p.m. by reservation; private groups of 10+ need about two weeks’ notice for a 10:00 a.m. tour—call 813-350-6553 for pricing.

## FAQ

### Where do the Tampa Buccaneers play?

Raymond James Stadium, 4201 N. Dale Mabry Hwy, Tampa, FL 33607. USF football and several bowls/concerts use the same building.

### What is the bag policy at Raymond James Stadium?

No bags except one clutch no larger than 4.5″ × 6.5″. It does not need to be clear. Larger clear bags are not allowed. Lockers are on Tom McEwen Blvd south of Suzuki Marine.

### Where is rideshare for a Bucs game?

George M. Steinbrenner Field for typical Buccaneers games. Concerts and high-volume nights can move pickup—read that event’s directions.

### Is parking a fixed price?

No. Lots are cashless; each event sets price and open time. Check the event listing and buy advance parking when offered.

### What is left on the 2026 home schedule after early November?

As of November 2, 2026: Monday Night Panthers (Nov 30), Chargers (Dec 6), Saints (Dec 20), and Rams (Week 17, time TBD), plus road games and TBD late-season slots. Confirm on buccaneers.com/schedule.

### Is Steinbrenner Field the Bucs stadium?

No. It is the rideshare/staging location for many Bucs games and a separate baseball complex on Dale Mabry.
