# Tampa Lightning Tickets: Official Sellers, Box Office, Rush

I went after Tampa Lightning tickets as a buying problem, not a team biography. The club is the Tampa Bay Lightning. The building is Benchmark International Arena, 401 Channelside Drive, Tampa, FL 33602. The old Amalie name still appears in stray email addresses and habits—that confusion is part of how people buy the wrong thing.

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026.

## Visit Snapshot

- **Official sellers:** Tampa Bay Lightning / Benchmark International Arena ReliaQuest Ticket Office and Ticketmaster
- **Ticket format:** Mobile only (Ticketmaster app entry)
- **Box office location:** West side of the arena; **event days only**
- **Lightning game window:** Opens 3 hours before puck drop; open through end of the second period
- **Concert / other shows:** Opens 3 hours before ticketed show time
- **Ticket office phone:** 813-301-2500
- **No refunds / no exchanges** on typical game, concert, or event tickets (cancellations/reschedules go back to original point of purchase)

Team schedule and ticket paths: [Lightning site](https://www.nhl.com/lightning). Arena events: [tampaarena.com](https://www.tampaarena.com/events/).

## Official vs Everything Else

The ReliaQuest ticket office states that the arena and the Lightning themselves are the only official sellers. Imposter sites are built to look affiliated. I never found a named official resale marketplace called out the way some MLB clubs point to a partner. If the seat is not in your team account or Ticketmaster through the club/arena path, assume you are the product.

All tickets are mobile. Delivery language includes mobile entry in the Ticketmaster app. Screenshots are a bad plan. If a show is canceled or rescheduled, you return to the original point of purchase—another reason lookalike sellers hurt.

## Box Office Reality

The office sits on the **west side** of the venue. It is open on event days only. Lightning games: three hours before puck drop through the end of the second period. Concerts and other shows: three hours before show time (closing point less clearly stated than the hockey window). Closed on major holidays unless an event is happening; holiday hours can change—call 813-301-2500. A random Tuesday with no event is not a walk-up shopping day.

**Cards:** Arena materials list Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. ReliaQuest ticket-office materials also list Discover. Same office, two lists—bring a card on both lists. I am not inventing service fees or face values; neither source locked a public price into the notes I used.

## Single-Game Schedule vs Ticket Packs

The club’s single-game ticket calendar runs month by month with home dates, times, and buy links. It does not reprint the arena name on every line—treat it as the home slate because that is the club’s own sales board.

**Clock conflicts are real.** Examples caught in 2026–27 materials:

- Some October home dates matched across single-game and pack pages (e.g., Flyers and Wild evenings at 7:00 p.m. ET in that snapshot)
- November 17 vs Washington: single-game page 7:00 p.m. ET vs pack page 7:30 p.m. ET
- March 25 vs Philadelphia: single-game 7:00 p.m. ET vs pack 6:30 p.m. ET

Same opponents, different clocks, both from Lightning properties. Believe the ticket you hold, then recheck the listing the week of the game. October 4, 2026, was not a home date on the single-game board in that early-season snapshot—the next home nights were October 5 (Flyers) and October 8 (Wild). “Tickets today” on an off day does not create a Channelside puck drop.

For season structure (84 games, homestands, Panthers dates), see [Tampa Bay Lightning](/events/tampa-bay-lightning).

## Student Rush and Other Discount Doors

**College student rush:** valid .edu address; standing room only unless the offer says otherwise; tickets drop at 10:00 a.m. on game days; non-transferable. No price published in the materials I used.

**High school students without .edu:** ReliaQuest Ticket Office on game day starting three hours before puck drop, with student ID, while supplies last. That window can end when tickets are gone—not necessarily when the second period ends.

GovX deals, theme nights, season-ticket interest forms, and group tickets also exist. Group offers in the notes came without a published minimum or price—ask sales directly rather than trusting a forwarded screenshot.

## Inside the Building (Ticket-Adjacent Rules)

Arena retail and concession stands are cashless on the hockey pattern, with mobile ordering at select stands. Concert merchandise may accept cash differently—hockey night and concert night are not the same wallet assumption. I did not get a bag-policy measurement from Lightning ticket notes; do not copy Raymond James’ 4.5×6.5 clutch rule or Tropicana’s clear-bag inches without checking the arena’s event page.

Presale codes and team-partner offers rotate through credit-card, radio, and membership lists. A code that worked for the home opener may be dead by midseason. If a social post promises ice-level seats below face, treat it as a scam drill until the checkout domain matches Ticketmaster or the club.

## Season Tickets, Groups, and Suites (Different Desks)

Individual game inventory and a full-season account are not the same phone call. Member services (813-301-6800) handles season seats. Groups and party areas use 813-301-6900. Suites go through 813-301-2505. Accessible seating is 813-301-6700. Calling the main arena line (813-301-6500) and expecting a will-call workaround fights the mobile-only rule. If you need ADA seats, start with the accessible line before you buy a standard inventory seat you cannot use.

Theme nights and giveaways attach to specific home dates. Those promotions do not change who is allowed to sell you the ticket. They also do not create refunds when you buy the wrong night from a reseller.

## Concert Tickets at the Same Window

Benchmark International Arena sells concert and family-show inventory through the same ReliaQuest office and Ticketmaster stack. The office opens three hours before show time for those events. Closing language is less tidy than the hockey “through the second period” rule—so do not assume you can buy a concert ticket at intermission the way a late hockey buyer might try before the second period ends. Hockey night cashless concessions and concert merch rules can differ; bring a card either way.

## Tips Before You Pay

- Buy from Ticketmaster via Lightning/arena paths or at the west-side ReliaQuest window on an event day.
- Load the pass in the Ticketmaster app—not a photo gallery.
- Ignore sellers that only look official.
- When pack times and single-game times disagree, trust the live ticket detail.
- Do not drive to Channelside for a “today” ticket on a date that is not on the home board.
- Call accessible seating (813-301-6700) before you assume a companion seat plan.
- Keep Bucs and Rays checkout tabs in separate browser sessions so venue maps do not blend.
- If an email still says Amalie in the footer, confirm the Channelside address before you panic—and still buy only through official paths.

## FAQ

### Who sells official Tampa Lightning tickets?

The Lightning / Benchmark International Arena (ReliaQuest Ticket Office) and Ticketmaster. The ticket office warns that other sellers posing as affiliates are not official.

### Where is the box office?

West side of Benchmark International Arena. Event days only. Hockey: three hours before puck drop through the second period.

### Can I get a paper ticket?

No. Tickets are mobile only.

### Are there refunds?

Standard game, concert, and event tickets: no refunds and no exchanges. If an event is canceled or rescheduled, work through the original seller.

### Is student rush available?

College rush uses a .edu workflow with morning drops on game days. High school students without .edu go to the ReliaQuest window with ID while supply lasts. Confirm current terms the day you try.

### Why does my pack list a different time than the schedule?

Lightning single-game and pack pages have disagreed on some 2026–27 clocks. Use the time printed on your ticket and recheck the club listing before you leave.

## A Simple Buying Checklist

1. Confirm the home date on the Lightning single-game board (not a random “Tampa tickets” ad).
2. Compare the listed time to any pack email you already hold—resolve conflicts before travel day.
3. Purchase through Ticketmaster or plan an event-day ReliaQuest visit with a Visa/MC/Amex (Discover only if you accept the risk of the shorter arena list).
4. Create or sign into the Ticketmaster account that will hold the pass.
5. Add accessible or group needs through the correct phone line before you finalize seats.
6. Screenshot nothing—open the live mobile entry at the door.
7. Recheck bag and entry notes on the arena event page the morning of the game.

If a seller cold-calls or DMs a “official Lightning inventory” link that is not Ticketmaster or the club/arena domain path, stop. The ReliaQuest fraud warning exists because those pages are designed to look close enough.

Transfer and resale behavior varies by offer—student rush is explicitly non-transferable in club language. Season-ticket holder transfer rules live in member materials, not in this visitor guide. When in doubt, ask member services before you buy a seat you cannot legally hand to a friend.
