Notes on a Saturday walk in Hyde Park

I walked South Howard (SoHo) on a Saturday morning in October 2026. Street parking filled by 10. I started at Old Hyde Park Village, 1602 W Swann Avenue, Tampa 33606. The village is open-air shops around a courtyard. I did not time the stores. I did not eat a sit-down breakfast; I got coffee and kept walking.

Bayshore Boulevard is the water. The sidewalk is wide. I walked south from the Hyde Park stretch toward Ballast Point and turned around after about 40 minutes. Bikes pass close. No admission. Shade is thin after 11.

I am not ranking restaurants on Howard. I am not calling anything a hidden anything. If someone wants a list of 10 restaurants I do not have one I trust. This note is the walk: village, Howard, Bayshore, parking, heat.
